### Executive Summary
A Civic owner installed a cheap aftermarket head unit from Amazon and used Claude to analyze it. The unit contained pre‑installed bloatware/spyware, including a voice assistant with WebView that consumed ~30% CPU even when idle, Baidu location services, a call recorder, a DAB radio client, and Adobe Acrobat. The findings highlight privacy and performance risks in aftermarket automotive electronics.
