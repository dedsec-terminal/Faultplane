### Executive Summary
Meta’s Muse AI, a new feature by Meta, collects and stores personal data—including friendships, arguments, and secrets—into detailed dossiers. The tool can profile users even if they never signed up, raising privacy concerns. Malwarebytes Labs reports that the AI’s data collection could be used for surveillance or targeted manipulation.
