### Executive Summary
CyberSN’s latest jobs report, compiled from JobSpikr data, shows a 26% drop in U.S. cybersecurity job postings while demand for AI security engineers rises sharply. The analysis maps titles to 45 functional roles across ten categories, emphasizing skill-based market shifts rather than company titles.
