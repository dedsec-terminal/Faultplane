### Executive Summary
A zero‑day vulnerability (CVE‑2025‑14713) in Synology DiskStation DS925+ C2 Identity Plugin allows unauthenticated network‑adjacent attackers to read sensitive information. The flaw is a dangerous method exposure that can disclose internal data. The vulnerability has a CVSS score of 6.5 and was disclosed by Zero Day Initiative.
