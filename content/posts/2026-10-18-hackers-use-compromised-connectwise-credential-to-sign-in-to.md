### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers report an active campaign where unknown threat actors abused a compromised ConnectWise Cloud Backup (formerly SkyKick) Microsoft 365 credential to gain unauthorized access to 173 customer M365 tenants. The attackers leveraged the credential to sign in and potentially move laterally within the tenants, posing a significant risk to MSP customers.
