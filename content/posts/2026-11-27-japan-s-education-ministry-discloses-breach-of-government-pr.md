### Executive Summary

Japan’s education ministry disclosed earlier this week that hackers had breached its government procurement system, potentially exposing records containing business information and email addresses. The Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) said it detected the intrusion on October 1, nearly two months before publicly disclosing the incident. An investigation found that hackers may have obtained information stored in the system’s database. The potentially compromised data includes nearly 1,700 email addresses and 164,000 records containing company names, the names of employees and other business information, the ministry said in a statement Wednesday.
