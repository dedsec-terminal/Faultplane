A cyberattack occurred at CEVA Logistics, the company responsible for shipping Steam hardware to customers in Europe, between July 29 and August 1, 2026. As a result, certain information about Steam customers was likely compromised, prompting Valve to send out data breach notification emails to affected customers. The breach was discovered on August 7, and CEVA is still investigating the incident.

The exposed information includes names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and order details, such as the type and price of the hardware. However, Valve assured customers that additional information related to their Steam account, payment information, passwords, and Steam Guard codes were not impacted. CEVA does not have access to this sensitive information, and the breach is limited to delivery information that CEVA needs to ship physical hardware to customers.

Valve is warning customers to be cautious of potential phishing attempts, where attackers may use the exposed information to send fake messages via email, SMS, or phone. The company is pushing CEVA for more details on the breach and is notifying data protection authorities in the affected countries. CEVA has taken steps to isolate the affected systems and has brought in outside investigators to aid in the investigation. Valve has advised customers to treat any suspicious messages as fake and has stated that no password change or account setting adjustment is needed at this time.
