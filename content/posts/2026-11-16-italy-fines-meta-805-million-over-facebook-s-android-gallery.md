### Executive Summary
Meta was fined €805 million by Italy’s data protection authority, Garante, for a default‑enabled Facebook Android feature that accessed users’ photos, videos and related metadata without explicit consent. The feature suggested media from users’ phone galleries for posting, violating privacy regulations. Meta has 60 days to comply.
