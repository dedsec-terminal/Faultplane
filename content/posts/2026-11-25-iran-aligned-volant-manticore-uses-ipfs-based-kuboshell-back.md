### Executive Summary
Volant Manticore, an Iran‑aligned cyber‑espionage group, has targeted telecoms, airlines and critical infrastructure in Cyprus. The group’s primary tool is the KuboShell backdoor, which uses the InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) for command‑and‑control and deploys the Homa post‑compromise framework, a reimplementation of a known tool. The operation demonstrates sophisticated use of decentralized storage for stealthy C2 and persistence.
