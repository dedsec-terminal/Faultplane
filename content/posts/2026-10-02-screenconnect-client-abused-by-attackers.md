### Executive Summary
Threat actors have been observed abusing the legitimate ScreenConnect remote‑control client to gain unauthorized access to victim systems. By leveraging the software’s remote‑control capabilities, attackers can execute commands, exfiltrate data, and maintain persistence without deploying custom malware. The incident demonstrates that attackers often exploit trusted applications rather than creating new threats.
