### Executive Summary
In the 2026 State of Agent Security Report, 1,280 third‑party AI‑embedded products were identified, 282 behind single sign‑on, while the rest remain invisible to identity infrastructure. The gap arises because identity stacks only govern authenticated agents, leaving many AI agents unmanaged and exposing organizations to unmonitored AI agents.
