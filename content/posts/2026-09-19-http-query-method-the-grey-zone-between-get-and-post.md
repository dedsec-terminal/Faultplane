### Executive Summary
In June 2026, the IETF published RFC 10008, introducing a new HTTP method called 'QUERY'. This method expands the HTTP verb set, following the last addition of 'PATCH' in 2010, and aims to provide a more flexible approach for server-client interactions beyond the traditional GET and POST methods.
