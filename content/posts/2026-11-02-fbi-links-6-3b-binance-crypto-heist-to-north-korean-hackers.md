The FBI confirmed that the $6.3 billion theft from Binance was carried out by the state‑sponsored North Korean hacking group TraderTraitor, making it the largest cryptocurrency heist on record. The investigation revealed that the attackers exploited Binance’s security to siphon funds from user accounts, and the FBI’s findings highlight the growing threat of nation‑state actors targeting digital asset exchanges.
