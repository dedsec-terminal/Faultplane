### Executive Summary
A remote information disclosure vulnerability (CVE-2026-114551) in MailEnable allows attackers to read sensitive data stored in cleartext without authentication. The Zero Day Initiative rated it CVSS 5.9. Exploitation requires no credentials, enabling attackers to access confidential information on affected installations.
