### Executive Summary
The China‑nexus cyber‑espionage group Volt Typhoon has been linked to the now‑disrupted Silent Train covert access network, which was used to launch four zero‑day exploitation campaigns. According to Lumen Technologies’ Black Lotus Labs, Silent Train operated via rotating chains of compromised SOHO, IoT, and enterprise infrastructure, enabling strategic access. The report, shared by The Hacker News, highlights the group’s use of zero‑day vulnerabilities to penetrate targets.
