### Executive Summary
A zero-day authentication bypass in United Chargers Grizzl‑E Smart 40A EV chargers’ OCPP backend allows network‑adjacent attackers to access the system without credentials. The flaw, identified by Zero Day Initiative as ZDI‑26‑1037, carries a CVSS score of 7.6 and is assigned CVE‑2026‑131809.

**Original Description:**
This vulnerability allows network-adjacent attackers to bypass authentication on affected installations of United Chargers Grizzl-E Smart 40A EV chargers. Authentication is not required to exploit this vulnerability. The ZDI has assigned a CVSS rating of 7.6. The following CVEs are assigned: CVE-2026-131809.
