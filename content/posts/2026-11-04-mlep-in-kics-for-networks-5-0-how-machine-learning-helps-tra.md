### Executive Summary
Kaspersky Industrial CyberSecurity for Networks (KICS) 5.0 introduces the Machine Learning Event Processor (MLEP) to automatically inventory OT devices and monitor industrial network traffic. MLEP analyzes packet flows, builds behavioral baselines, and flags deviations that may indicate misconfigurations or malicious activity. The solution enhances visibility into OT environments and supports proactive anomaly detection.
