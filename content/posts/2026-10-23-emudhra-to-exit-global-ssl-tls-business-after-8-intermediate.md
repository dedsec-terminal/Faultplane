### Executive Summary
Indian certificate authority eMudhra announced it will withdraw from its global SSL/TLS certificate business after a researcher recovered the private keys of eight intermediate CA certificates, which were easily guessable. The CA will replace affected customers’ certificates with free ones from partners.
