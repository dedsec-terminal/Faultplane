### Executive Summary

A single successful Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) logon to an internet‑exposed system in early March 2025 marked the beginning of an intrusion that is believed to have deployed the Lynx ransomware. The attack did not involve credential stuffing, brute‑force attempts, or other failed authentication events from the source IP, suggesting a targeted or credential‑stolen approach. While the article does not detail the full extent of data loss or encryption, it implies that the ransomware likely encrypted victim files. No specific defensive guidance is provided in the excerpt, though the incident underscores the importance of securing RDP access.
