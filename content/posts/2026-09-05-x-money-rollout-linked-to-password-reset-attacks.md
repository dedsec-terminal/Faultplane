### Executive Summary
X's new payments service, X Money, has triggered a wave of unsolicited password‑reset emails. Attackers likely spoof X's domain to trick users into resetting credentials, potentially compromising accounts. Users should verify email authenticity, avoid clicking links, and enable two‑factor authentication.
