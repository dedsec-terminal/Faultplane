### Executive Summary
EvilTokens has emerged as a leading PhaaS platform that facilitates device code phishing by using AI‑generated lures, automated infrastructure, and token theft. Microsoft Digital Crimes Unit (DCU) partnered with other entities to disrupt EvilTokens’ infrastructure and operations, effectively shutting down its phishing capabilities.
