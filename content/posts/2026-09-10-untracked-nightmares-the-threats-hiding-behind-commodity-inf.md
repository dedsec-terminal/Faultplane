### Executive Summary
Unit 42's investigation reveals that cybercriminals leveraged YouTube gaming videos and SEO poisoning to lure victims, delivering a suite of malware payloads into enterprise networks. The operation exploited commodity infrastructure, enabling attackers to distribute multi‑payload malware at scale while remaining hidden behind legitimate-looking content.
