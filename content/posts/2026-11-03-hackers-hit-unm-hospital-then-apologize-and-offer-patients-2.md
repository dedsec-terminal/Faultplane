### Executive Summary
Ransomware actors breached the University of New Mexico hospital, then issued an apology and offered $2,500 to each affected patient. In a follow‑up statement, they disclosed additional details about the attack, including the scope of data accessed and the steps taken to mitigate the breach. The move—providing direct financial compensation to patients—is atypical for threat actors and highlights the evolving tactics of ransomware groups.
