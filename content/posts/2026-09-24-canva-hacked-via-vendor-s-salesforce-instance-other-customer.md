### Executive Summary
Canva was breached through a compromised Salesforce instance belonging to a vendor. The attacker group, The Seven Deadly Sins, targeted the platform on Aug 28, demanding payment. The breach exposed customer data and impacted other clients of the vendor’s Salesforce environment. DataBreaches.net reported the incident and identified the group’s new leak site.
