Some conversations on the Claude chat platform, developed by Anthropic, have been found to be searchable on Google. These conversations contain personal and sensitive information, including private cryptocurrency wallet keys, addresses, and medical billing data. The exposed data also includes notes from meetings and a dashboard for analyzing medical billing data, as well as information from an AI-powered therapy app.

The issue appears to be related to a user setting that allows conversations to be shared publicly. According to Anthropic, users have control over sharing their conversations, and the company does not share chat directories or sitemaps with search engines like Google. However, when a user chooses to share a conversation, it becomes publicly accessible and may be archived by third-party services. Anthropic's position is that it is not their responsibility, as users are making the content publicly accessible when they share it.

To address the issue, users can review their settings to ensure that their conversations are not being shared publicly. It is essential for users to be aware of the potential risks of sharing sensitive information online and to take steps to protect their personal data. By being mindful of their settings and taking necessary precautions, users can help prevent their conversations from being searchable on Google and protect their sensitive information from being exposed.
