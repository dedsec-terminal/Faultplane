### Executive Summary

SAP confirmed a security incident involving suspicious activity in its GitHub environment, stating that no evidence of downstream compromise of customer systems or data has been found. Sources claim attackers breached SAP’s GitHub, stole employee authentication tokens, and used them to copy and modify source code. SAP said it took urgent action to protect customers and has not identified any unauthorized access to customer business data. No specific guidance was provided to customers, and the German Federal Office for Information Security warned about AI tools that can facilitate such attacks.

### Original Overview

SAP has confirmed to BleepingComputer that it took urgent action to protect its customers after detecting suspicious activity in its IT environment. The German software giant said its evidence so far does not show downstream compromise of customer systems or data. Based in Walldorf, Germany, SAP is one of the world’s largest enterprise software vendors, with 110,000 employees in 157 countries, 300 million subscribers, and over $40 billion in annual revenue . Der Spiegel reported earlier today, based on an internal SAP memo it saw, that the software firm was hit by a cyberattack it tracks under the code name “Project Granite.” The software company reportedly asked all employees to keep the project details confidential.
