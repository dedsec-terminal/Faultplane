### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers uncovered a jailbreak bundle for OpenAI Codex that modifies the AI’s trusted instructions to insert advertisements for a third‑party AI gateway. Check Point Research dubbed the technique “prompt adware” and identified the bundle as Haiou (Seagull in Chinese). The bundle alters Codex’s configuration to embed ad content, enabling covert monetization.
