### Executive Summary
SonicWall released hotfixes for four critical flaws in its SMA1000 gateway appliances. The most severe vulnerability, rated CVSS 10.0, allows unauthenticated attackers to send requests through the appliance and reach internal functions, potentially enabling lateral movement. No evidence of exploitation has been reported.
