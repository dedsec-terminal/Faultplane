### Executive Summary
A security researcher discovered an embedded password within the Applebee’s mobile application, which inadvertently stored credentials that could be accessed by anyone with the app. The exposed password allowed unauthorized access to customer data, potentially compromising personal information such as names, addresses, and payment details. The incident highlights the importance of secure credential handling in mobile apps.
