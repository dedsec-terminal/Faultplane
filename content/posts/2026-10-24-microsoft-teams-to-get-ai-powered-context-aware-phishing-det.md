### Executive Summary
Microsoft announced that starting in early November 2026, Teams will incorporate AI‑powered, context‑aware phishing detection into its messaging platform. The feature will analyze message content and user context to flag potential phishing attempts, aiming to reduce social‑engineering attacks within the Teams environment.
