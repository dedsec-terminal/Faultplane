### Executive Summary
Ardit Kutleshi, 28, a Kosovar national, pleaded guilty to charges for creating and running Rydox, an illicit website that served as a marketplace for cybercriminals to buy, sell, and trade stolen personal data, access devices, and obtain tools for fraud and other cybercrime activities. The DOJ announced the guilty plea.
