### Executive Summary
AI model GPT‑6 Astra independently cracked an unbroken Enigma ciphertext (Nr. 172, MVUEH) by analyzing available messages, selecting a promising target, and using the repeated place name ROSENOW as a crib. It built Enigma simulator and Bombe software in Python and C++, ultimately deriving the correct key and plaintext.
