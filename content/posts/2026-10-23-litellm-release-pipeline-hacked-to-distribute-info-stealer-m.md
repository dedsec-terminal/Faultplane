### Executive Summary
Hackers compromised the LiteLLM open‑source project’s PyPI package and pushed malicious Docker images that contain an info‑stealer. The payload harvests credentials, secrets, and environment variables from the host, then exfiltrates them. The attack demonstrates how supply‑chain attacks can weaponize legitimate package repositories and container registries.
