### Executive Summary
BragJack, a proof‑of‑concept attack by Forever Security’s Gal Weizman, hijacks AI assistants in Chrome, Edge, Opera Neon, Perplexity Comet, and Claude in Chrome by installing a single malicious extension. The Prompt Forcing technique earned over $20,000 in bounties and led to two CVEs.
