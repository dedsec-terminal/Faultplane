### Executive Summary
Apple released a comprehensive security update on November 10, 2026, addressing multiple high‑severity vulnerabilities across macOS, iOS, iPadOS, and Apple WatchOS. The update includes fixes for several CVEs, mitigates zero‑day exploits, and strengthens kernel and application sandboxing. Apple recommends users install the update immediately to protect against potential remote code execution and data‑exfiltration attacks.
