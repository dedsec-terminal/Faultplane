### Executive Summary
A stack-based buffer overflow in Cisco Snort’s OLE VBA decompression routine allows unauthenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary code. The Zero Day Initiative identified the flaw (ZDI-26-788) and assigned CVE-2026-20218, with a CVSS score of 8.1.

This vulnerability allows remote attackers to execute arbitrary code on affected installations of Cisco Snort. Authentication is not required to exploit this vulnerability. The ZDI has assigned a CVSS rating of 8.1. The following CVEs are assigned: CVE-2026-20218.
