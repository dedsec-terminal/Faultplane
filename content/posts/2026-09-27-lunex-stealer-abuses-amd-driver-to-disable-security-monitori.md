### Executive Summary
Lunex Stealer, a malware-as-a-service (MaaS) tool, targets Ukrainian-speaking users through a four‑stage attack chain. It begins with a fake CAPTCHA page that redirects to a ClickFix‑style Cloudflare verification, then drops a malicious AMD driver to disable security monitoring, and finally steals browser credentials. The malware is distributed via compromised Ukrainian websites and leverages the driver to evade detection.
