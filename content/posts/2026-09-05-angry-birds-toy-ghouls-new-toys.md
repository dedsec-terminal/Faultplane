### Executive Summary
Kaspersky GERT researchers uncovered two new backdoor variants deployed by the Toy Ghouls threat group. One variant uses the HiveMQ MQTT broker as its command‑and‑control (C2) server, while the other leverages the Matrix-based Element messenger for C2 communications.
