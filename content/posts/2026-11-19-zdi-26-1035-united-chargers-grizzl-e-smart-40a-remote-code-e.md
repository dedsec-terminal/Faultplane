### Executive Summary
The Zero Day Initiative (ZDI) disclosed CVE-2026-131810, a remote code execution flaw in United Chargers Grizzl‑E Smart 40A electric vehicle chargers. The vulnerability allows network‑adjacent attackers to download and execute arbitrary code without authentication or integrity checks. The flaw carries a CVSS score of 7.5 and was identified during a Pwn2Own event.
