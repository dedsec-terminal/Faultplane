### Executive Summary
After a major data breach at French telecom operator Free Mobile, attackers sent highly convincing phishing emails to customers, mimicking official communications. The emails aimed to harvest credentials and personal data. Malwarebytes Labs identified the campaign and warned users to verify sender authenticity and avoid clicking links.
