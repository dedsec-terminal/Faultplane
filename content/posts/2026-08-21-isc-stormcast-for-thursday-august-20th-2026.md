### Executive Summary
The SANS Internet Storm Center released its daily stormcast for Thursday, August 20, 2026, summarizing the latest global network anomalies, emerging threats, and notable incidents. The report highlighted increased activity from botnet command‑and‑control servers, a spike in phishing campaigns targeting financial institutions, and several new malware samples detected in the wild. Analysts also noted a rise in DNS tunneling attempts and a new exploit targeting a widely used web application framework. The ISC team urged organizations to review their security posture and apply relevant patches.
