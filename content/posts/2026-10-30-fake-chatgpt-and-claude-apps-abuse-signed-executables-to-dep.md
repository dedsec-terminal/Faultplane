### Executive Summary
Threat actors are distributing malicious loaders and cryptocurrency miners disguised as AI desktop applications for Anthropic Claude and OpenAI ChatGPT. They create fake GitHub projects (e.g., GPT‑5.6 Sol, GPT‑6 Astra, Claude Opus) that host signed executables, enabling the malware family to bypass security checks. The campaign, dubbed Yoba, exploits the trust users place in AI tools.
