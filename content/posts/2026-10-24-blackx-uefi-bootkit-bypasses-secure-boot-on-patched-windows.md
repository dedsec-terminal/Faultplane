### Executive Summary
A new UEFI bootkit, BlackX, has targeted diplomatic organizations in Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, and Turkmenistan in early 2026. The malware bypasses Secure Boot on patched Windows systems that lack revocation updates. Kaspersky attributes the operation to the China‑linked state‑sponsored group APT41. The attack demonstrates advanced firmware exploitation and highlights gaps in Windows revocation mechanisms.

Diplomatic organizations in Central Asia have been targeted by a sophisticated new Unified Extensible Firmware Interface (UEFI) bootkit called BlackX.
