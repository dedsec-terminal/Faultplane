### Executive Summary
A vulnerability in TeamViewer allows a compromised host to crash or potentially execute code on the viewer’s machine during a remote session. The flaw, fixed by the vendor, could let attackers disrupt or compromise the controlling computer. The patch addresses the issue across platforms.
