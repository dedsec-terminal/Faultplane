### Executive Summary
Quest Diagnostics reported a breach that exposed personal information and patient records. The incident compromised sensitive data such as names, dates of birth, medical conditions, and test results. Patients are advised to monitor accounts, review statements, and consider credit monitoring services. Quest is investigating and will notify affected individuals.
