### Executive Summary
CERT Polska warns that attackers exploit MikroTik routers’ internet‑exposed SSH service to gain full administrative control without authentication. The attacks began at least by September 2, with no confirmed victim count reported by Hacker News. The vulnerability allows remote takeover of the routers’ management interface.
