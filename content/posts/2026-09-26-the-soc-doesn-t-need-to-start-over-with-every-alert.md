### Executive Summary
AI has lowered the cost of retrying failed cyberattacks. Attackers now land on low‑privilege cloud accounts, attempt privilege escalation, and if it fails they can quickly retry using AI‑driven automation. Previously, each dead end required hours of manual research; AI enables rapid iteration, making the attack process cheaper and faster.
