### Executive Summary
The LiteLLM Python package (v1.107.0) was compromised in a supply‑chain attack that injected a credential‑stealing module and a backdoor. The malicious code was distributed via PyPI wheels and Docker images. The package has been quarantined on PyPI. The incident was reported by SafeDep, Socket, StepSecurity, and Upwind.
