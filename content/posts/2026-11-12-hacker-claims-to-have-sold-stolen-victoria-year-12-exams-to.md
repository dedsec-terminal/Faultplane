### Executive Summary
A hacker breached the Victorian Curriculum and Assessment Authority (VCAA) website, posting a taunting message and claiming to have obtained and sold this year’s Year 12 exam papers to students. The breach exposed sensitive exam materials, raising concerns about academic integrity and data security.
