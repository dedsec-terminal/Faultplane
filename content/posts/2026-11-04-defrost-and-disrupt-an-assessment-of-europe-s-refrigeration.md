### Executive Summary
On Nov. 2-3, fridges and freezers in six European countries were warmed overnight, disrupting operations. SentinelOne Labs attributes the attacks to Russian‑aligned threat actors, though attribution remains open. The incidents highlight vulnerabilities in industrial control systems and the growing threat to critical infrastructure.
