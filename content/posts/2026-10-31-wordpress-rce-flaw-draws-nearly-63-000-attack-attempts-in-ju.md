### Executive Summary
A high‑severity remote code execution flaw in WordPress Core (v4.7‑7.1.3) with a CVSS score of 8.1, lacking a CVE ID, triggered almost 63,000 exploitation attempts within 24 hours of patch release in October. Security teams observed widespread probing as attackers sought to exploit the vulnerability before the official fix was applied.
