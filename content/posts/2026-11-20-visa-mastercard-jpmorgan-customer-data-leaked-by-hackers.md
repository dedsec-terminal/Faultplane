### Executive Summary
Thousands of customers of Visa, Mastercard, and JPMorgan Chase had their data exposed in a breach involving a third‑party service provider. The incident did not affect payment card numbers or Visa’s own systems, but it raised concerns about the security of customer information handled by external vendors.
