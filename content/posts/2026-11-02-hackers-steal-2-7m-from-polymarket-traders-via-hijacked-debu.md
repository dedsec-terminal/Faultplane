### Executive Summary
Malicious code hijacked a page‑speed script on Polymarket’s website, selling signed‑in traders’ positions and transferring about $2.7 million in crypto to attackers on Oct 29. Polymarket is refunding roughly $2.7 million to 726 affected wallets. The breach involved a compromised DebugBear script.
