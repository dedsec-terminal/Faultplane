### Executive Summary
Coinbase halted $4.7 million worth of crypto transfers to scammers who promoted a fake giveaway using an AI‑generated deepfake of Elon Musk during a Tesla livestream that drew over 500,000 viewers. The platform flagged the fraudulent activity and prevented the funds from reaching the scammers.
