Authorities in Ukraine and Russia reported that their emergency alert mobile apps were targeted by cyberattacks, causing users to receive false warnings of an imminent nuclear threat. The attacks occurred as a limited ceasefire ended, but it is unclear whether they are connected. The incidents highlight the vulnerability of critical communication systems during conflict.
