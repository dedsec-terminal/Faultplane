### Executive Summary
North Korean threat actors used a job‑interview lure that delivered malicious Terraform lock files to a DevOps engineer’s Mac, establishing a foothold and installing the TraderTraitor backdoor. The malware, which can call external APIs, reappears on victims even when they have no cryptocurrency involvement, indicating a broader espionage or sabotage agenda.
