### Executive Summary
Apple has introduced Reference Image, a system that verifies an image’s authenticity and source without linking it to a specific iPhone or photographer. The system signs images with Apple’s signing service after validation by PCC, enabling verification that multiple photos came from the same device while preserving photographer anonymity. This addresses concerns for conflict‑zone photographers who need to prove authenticity without revealing identity.
