### Executive Summary
OpenAI announced it will double the usage limits for its Pro plan and integrate ChatGPT Work features directly into the standard Chat interface. The update also merges usage allowances across ChatGPT and Codex, allowing users to share their token quota between the two services. This move aims to streamline workflow and improve productivity for professional users.
