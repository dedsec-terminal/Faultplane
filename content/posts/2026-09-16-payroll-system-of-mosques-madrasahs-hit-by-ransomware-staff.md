### Executive Summary
The payroll system of mosques and madrasahs overseen by Singapore's Islamic Religious Council (MUIS) was compromised by ransomware. The SmartHRMS HR system, supplied by Avelogic, was targeted, potentially exposing staff personal data. The incident was reported by The Straits Times and noted on Avelogic's website.
