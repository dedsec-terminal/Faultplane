### Executive Summary
CERT/CC warns that Skullcandy Dime 3 earbuds automatically accept Bluetooth pairing requests from nearby unpaired devices, enabling attackers to hijack connections without user interaction. The vulnerability allows malicious devices to connect and potentially intercept audio or inject commands. Users should disable Bluetooth or update firmware, and manufacturers should enforce pairing authentication.
