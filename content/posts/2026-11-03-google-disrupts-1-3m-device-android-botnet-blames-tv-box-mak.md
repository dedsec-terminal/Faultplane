### Executive Summary
Google dismantled a botnet of over 1.3 million Android TV set‑top boxes that had lost control of its command servers in late October. The company claims the manufacturer, Videostrong, deliberately installed a backdoor and sold access to criminals, who used the devices for ad fraud and to route other users’ traffic.
