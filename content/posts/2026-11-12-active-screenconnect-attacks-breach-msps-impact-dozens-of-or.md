### Executive Summary
ConnectWise is investigating an active malware campaign that targets managed service providers (MSPs) by compromising ScreenConnect remote‑access environments. Attackers deploy rogue remote‑access clients to customer devices, breaching dozens of organizations. The campaign leverages compromised ScreenConnect instances to spread malware and gain persistence.
