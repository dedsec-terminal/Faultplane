### Executive Summary
Reports indicate multiple U.S. military bases experienced refrigeration outages, suspected to be cyberattacks. Affected installations include Fort Irwin, F.E. Warren AFB, Fort Huachuca, Naval Station Newport, Columbus AFB, Travis AFB, and Naval Air Station Lemoore. Bases declined to confirm details; Pentagon remains silent.
