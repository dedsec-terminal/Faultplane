### Executive Summary
Epichealth, an Australian medical clinic operator, reported a cyber incident on 4 Nov 2026. An unauthorized third party accessed part of its IT systems. The breach involved four clinics: Warrnambool Medicare Urgent Care, Ocean Grove Marketplace, Ocean Plaza, and Portarlington. Epichealth notified affected parties and is investigating the incident.
