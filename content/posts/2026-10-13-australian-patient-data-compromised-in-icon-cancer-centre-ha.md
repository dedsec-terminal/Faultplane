### Executive Summary
The Icon Cancer Centre, Australia's largest cancer care provider, suffered a data breach that exposed patient information. The breach involved unauthorized access to data stored on a supplier’s system, affecting patients across the country. The operator confirmed the incident and is investigating the extent of the compromise.
