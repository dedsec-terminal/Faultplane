### Executive Summary
China‑linked ransomware group Warlock exploited SharePoint vulnerabilities to gain initial access and compromise a water utility, a telecom provider, a regional government body, and a university. The attacks leveraged the same SharePoint flaw to move laterally and encrypt data, demanding ransom payments.
