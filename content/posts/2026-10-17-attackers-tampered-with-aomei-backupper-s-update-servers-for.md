### Executive Summary
Between May and July 2026, attackers repeatedly altered files on AOMEI Backupper’s official update servers for 5‑10 minute windows. Any Windows backup client that checked for updates during those windows downloaded and executed a backdoor. The operation was detected by Google Threat Intelligence, Mandiant, and ManageEngine’s Threat Analysis.
