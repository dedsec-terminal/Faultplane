### Executive Summary
European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen proposed an emergency mechanism allowing any EU country to summon the bloc’s governments in response to security threats such as sabotage, cyberattacks, and drone incursions. The proposal was announced during her State of the Union address in Strasbourg, highlighting the mounting threats to EU security.
