### Executive Summary
Sweden’s data privacy regulator IMY fined IT systems provider Miljödata SEK 1.8 million ($183,000) for inadequate security measures that caused a breach in August 2025. The incident exposed personal data of 2.2 million people, prompting the regulator’s enforcement action.
