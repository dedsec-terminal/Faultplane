### Executive Summary
Japanese cloud and data‑center provider Sakura Internet confirmed that attackers accessed its sales‑management system, compromising customer contract and membership records for up to 1.36 million accounts. The breach, discovered in early 2024, involved unauthorized read access to sensitive data but no evidence of financial theft or credential misuse. The company notified affected customers and is investigating the intrusion vector.
