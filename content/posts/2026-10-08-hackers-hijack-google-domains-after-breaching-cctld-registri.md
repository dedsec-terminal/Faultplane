### Executive Summary
Hackers compromised operators of Ghana, American Samoa, and Sierra Leone ccTLD registries, stole unauthorized HTTPS certificates for Google domains, and altered authoritative DNS records to hijack those domains. The attack leveraged third‑party registry access to redirect traffic, potentially enabling phishing or data interception.
