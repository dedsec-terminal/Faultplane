### Executive Summary
A recent phishing campaign used a fake login page that not only captured user credentials but also queried the victim's MX records to gather additional email infrastructure data. The attack was observed by SANS Internet Storm Center, highlighting the evolving tactics of phishing attempts beyond simple credential harvesting.
