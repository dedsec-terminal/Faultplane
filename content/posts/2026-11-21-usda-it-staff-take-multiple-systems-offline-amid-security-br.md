### Executive Summary
The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) experienced a cybersecurity incident that forced IT staff to take several applications offline over multiple days. The outages affected financial tools and other critical systems, disrupting operations. The breach was internally disclosed earlier this week and was reported by three sources and an email viewed by Nextgov/FCW and Government Executive.
