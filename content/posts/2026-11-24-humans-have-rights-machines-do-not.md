### Executive Summary
In 2024, Character.AI filed a motion to dismiss a wrongful‑death lawsuit alleging its chatbot caused a teen’s suicide. The company argued its responses were protected speech and that it was a service, not a product, thus exempt from product‑liability law. Judge Anne Conway rejected both defenses in May 2025, holding the company liable. The case highlights legal challenges surrounding AI‑driven chatbots and liability for user harm.
