### Executive Summary
Arista issued an advisory on Oct 22 that a zero‑day SQL injection flaw in its VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO) is being exploited. The vulnerability allows anyone who can log in—even with a read‑only customer account—to gain operator privileges, the highest level of control. Attackers are using this flaw to compromise SD‑WAN networks.
