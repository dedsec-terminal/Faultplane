### Executive Summary
The New York Times reports on the growing use of AI-powered surveillance systems in baby monitoring devices, such as Nanit. These devices collect extensive data on infants’ speech, language, motor skills, and health, aiming to provide 24/7 tracking that extends into early adolescence. Nanit recently secured $50 million to expand AI capabilities and broaden its presence in children’s bedrooms, raising concerns about privacy and data security.
