### Executive Summary
The eMudhra certificate authority had eight intermediate certificates revoked after researchers discovered their private keys were extremely weak, allowing instant recovery and forging of trusted certificates for legitimate sites. In response, Mozilla, Google, and Apple blocked the compromised certificates to prevent malicious use.
