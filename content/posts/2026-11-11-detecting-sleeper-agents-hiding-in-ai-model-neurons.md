### Executive Summary
Microsoft Research released threat research showing a capable agent model can be turned into a sleeper agent by editing fewer than ten neurons to inject a short payload. The research also demonstrates that frontier agents can detect this threat by identifying anomalous weight edits. The findings highlight new risks in AI model integrity and detection techniques.
