### Executive Summary
Three Russian threat clusters—UNC6293, UNC7005, and UNC5976—have been observed exploiting legitimate Google OAuth and WhatsApp linking flows to target individuals in academia, aerospace, defense, governments, and think tanks across Europe and the U.S. The groups employ persistent, adaptive techniques to hijack accounts and facilitate espionage activities.
