### Executive Summary

FinCEN reported a cybersecurity incident that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems. In response, the bureau took some systems offline as a precautionary measure. No further impact or guidance was disclosed.

### Original Overview

The U.S. Treasury Department's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) has been hit by a cybersecurity incident that resulted in "unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems," the bureau said on Tuesday.
