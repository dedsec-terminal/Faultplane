### Executive Summary
Hackers took control of software regulating temperature in older Danfoss-connected refrigeration units at a French supermarket chain, forcing some units to heat to 50°C overnight. The attack exploited unpatchable security software, causing the fridges to remain hot and potentially spoil food.
