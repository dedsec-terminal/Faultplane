### Executive Summary
A policy in Wapello County, Iowa, instructs police to keep the use of Flock license‑plate reader cameras secret, prohibiting mention of ALPR usage to vehicle occupants, in reports, or complaints unless absolutely necessary. The directive mirrors past tactics used to conceal IMSI‑catcher deployment, raising concerns about transparency and privacy.
