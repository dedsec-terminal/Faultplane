### Executive Summary

On May 27, 2026, California’s Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI) discovered that some grant, scholarship, and loan applications submitted through its Funding Portal 1.0 could be accessed via certain search engine links. The agency mailed notification letters to affected individuals on November 16, 2026, informing them of the potential exposure of their application data. No additional defensive guidance was provided in the source beyond the notification. The incident highlights a vulnerability in the portal’s access controls that allowed unintended public visibility of sensitive application information.
