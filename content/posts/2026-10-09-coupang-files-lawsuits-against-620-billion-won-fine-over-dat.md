### Executive Summary
In December 2025, South Korea’s largest online retailer Coupang apologized for a November data breach that exposed personal information of over 33 million customers. The incident prompted a 620 billion won fine from regulators. Coupang has now filed lawsuits against the fine, citing procedural errors and seeking to reduce the penalty. The case highlights challenges in incident response and regulatory compliance for e‑commerce platforms.
