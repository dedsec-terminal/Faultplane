### Executive Summary
Unit 42 reports that eight eMudhra intermediate CA private keys have been compromised, enabling attackers to sign TLS certificates and perform man‑in‑the‑middle attacks. The breach exposes a large number of certificates and could allow widespread MitM interception. The article includes analysis of the impact and containment guidance.
