### Executive Summary
SilkParasite, a cyber‑espionage operation first identified in late 2025, targets government bodies across Central Asia. The intrusion set deploys seven remote access tool (RAT) families, five of which are newly documented: DriveSilkRAT, CookiETagRAT, NomadRAT, GoginRAT, and NodeEdgeRAT. The campaign demonstrates advanced persistence and lateral movement capabilities, posing a significant threat to regional state security.
