### Executive Summary

The article explains that phishing attacks must bypass email security, web filtering, and safe browsing to reach employees. It highlights that certain phishing kits achieve higher breakthrough rates, meaning they are more successful at evading these controls. Push Security measures this success as a breakthrough rate metric, defined as the share of attacks that get past email security, web gateways, and safe browsing to reach the user. The piece advises security teams to focus on kits with high breakthrough rates to better defend against phishing threats.

### Original Overview

Every phishing attack has to get past email security, web filtering, and safe browsing before it reaches an employee.
