### Executive Summary
Check Point Security Gateway devices were compromised through the PHP2Shell vulnerability, allowing attackers to upload and execute a data‑wiping payload on connected VMware ESXi hosts. The malware deletes virtual machine files, causing significant data loss. The attack demonstrates the risk of unpatched gateway firmware and the need for strict network segmentation.
