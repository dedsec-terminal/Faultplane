### Executive Summary
WorkOS, a developer platform, reported a data breach where a threat actor exploited the ARTEX AI tool to gain access to its internal sales application. The attacker accessed business contact records and summaries of customer communications, underscoring the risks of third‑party AI tools and internal application security.
