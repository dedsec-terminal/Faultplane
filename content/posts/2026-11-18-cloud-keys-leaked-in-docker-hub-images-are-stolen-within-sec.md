### Executive Summary
Credentials and access secrets embedded in public Docker Hub images are being extracted and exploited by attackers within as little as eight seconds of publication. The leak exposes cloud keys, allowing attackers to gain unauthorized access to cloud resources. The incident highlights the risk of storing secrets in public container images and the rapid exploitation window.
