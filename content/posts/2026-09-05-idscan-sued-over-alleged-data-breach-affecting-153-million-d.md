### Executive Summary
Identity verification firm IDScan is facing multiple lawsuits after hackers allegedly breached its system and offered to sell data on more than 153 million U.S. driver’s licenses. The lawsuits claim the breach exposed personal information, including names, addresses, license numbers and dates of birth, potentially enabling identity theft. IDScan has denied wrongdoing and is contesting the claims.
