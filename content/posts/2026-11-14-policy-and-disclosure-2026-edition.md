### Executive Summary
Project Zero’s 90+30 day vulnerability disclosure policy is now insufficient due to AI‑driven rapid vulnerability discovery and exploitation. The policy gives vendors 90 days to patch plus 30 days for rollout, but large language models accelerate attacker capabilities, raising immediate risk. The article argues vendors must shorten remediation timelines to protect users.

**Original Description:**
Since 2021, Project Zero has followed a “90 + 30” vulnerability disclosure policy, allowing vendors up to 90 days to remediate a vulnerability plus an extra 30 days “soak time” for patch rollout. Our goal was to encourage faster patching and give vendors time to deliver patches to users. The advent of AI-assisted vulnerability discovery and exploitation has changed the threat landscape such that the vulnerability remediation timeline encouraged by our disclosure policy is clearly insufficient...
