Zenity has announced a significant expansion of its AI security platform, introducing a new security architecture designed to govern AI decisions before they become enterprise actions. The platform adds Exposure Management and Runtime Boundaries to its existing foundation, allowing organizations to enforce security at the decision point where an AI agent makes decisions, before it acts. This is particularly important for autonomous AI, which introduces a new challenge of governing long-horizon agents that operate over extended periods and make decisions that can have unintended consequences.

The Zenity AI security platform introduces security at the decision layer, evaluating every AI action before it becomes an enterprise action. This approach provides continuous control over what AI is allowed to do, rather than relying on alerts after an AI agent has already acted. The platform is organized around three integrated capabilities: Surface, Enforce, and Protect. Enforce applies Runtime Boundaries to evaluate every AI action in real-time, determining whether it should proceed, be blocked, or be terminated before business impact occurs. Runtime Boundaries serve as the decision engine for autonomous AI, continuously evaluating every AI decision in the context of the agent's broader execution history.

The introduction of Runtime Boundaries gives enterprises precise control over autonomous AI, allowing them to define what AI agents are permitted to do and enforcing those decisions consistently across various AI agents. According to Tomer Teller, VP of Product at Zenity, the future of AI security will be defined by what AI is allowed to do before it acts, rather than what an AI agent has already done. The Zenity AI security platform creates a continuous AI security loop where exposure informs runtime decisions, investigations improve policies, and every decision strengthens future protection. This approach enables organizations to stay ahead of emerging risks and ensure the secure and responsible use of autonomous AI.
