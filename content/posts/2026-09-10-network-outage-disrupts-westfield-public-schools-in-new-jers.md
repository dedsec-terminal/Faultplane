### Executive Summary
Westfield Public Schools in New Jersey suffered a districtwide network outage during the first week of school, disrupting communications and digital instruction. The district initially blamed a networking hardware failure, but evidence later revealed that a ransomware group had posted sample files, indicating a cyberattack was the underlying cause.
