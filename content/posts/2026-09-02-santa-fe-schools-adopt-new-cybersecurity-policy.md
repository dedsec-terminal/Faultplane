### Executive Summary
The Santa Fe school board approved a new cybersecurity policy this week, aiming to strengthen digital defenses across the district. However, a separate draft policy (Draft 357) addressing student data privacy was postponed for further debate, with most members citing its broad scope and the need for careful consideration. The board’s decision reflects a cautious approach to balancing security measures with privacy concerns.
