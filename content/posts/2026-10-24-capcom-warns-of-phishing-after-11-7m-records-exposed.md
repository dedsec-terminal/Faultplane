### Executive Summary
Capcom, the maker of Resident Evil, has warned that an exposure of 11.7 million email addresses could enable phishing attacks against its CAPCOM ID users. The breach also involved unauthorized password changes on 196 accounts. The incident was reported by Malwarebytes Labs.
