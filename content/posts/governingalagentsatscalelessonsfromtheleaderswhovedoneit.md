Enterprise AI leaders from ZoomInfo, Docusign, and AppViewX shared their experiences in building AI Centers of Excellence and governing agent identities at scale. The panel discussion, moderated by Alex Babar, aimed to provide insights into the daily operations of AI Centers of Excellence at ZoomInfo and Docusign. The leaders shared their approaches to governing agent identities without creating a parallel identity stack, highlighting the challenges they faced and the lessons they learned.

The panelists, including Sadeq Zabihi from Docusign, Venkat Chivukula from ZoomInfo, and Archit Lohokare and Kashyap Ivaturi from AppViewX, discussed the evolution of Agent Identity Security and its importance in achieving trusted AI. They emphasized the need to get Agent Identity Security right and shared their experiences in avoiding common traps and pitfalls. The discussion also touched on the concept of trusted AI and its implications for product decisions, providing valuable insights for peers just getting started with AI governance.

The key takeaways from the panel discussion included the importance of building a robust AI Center of Excellence, governing agent identities effectively, and avoiding common mistakes. The panelists offered advice for their peers, including the need to prioritize Agent Identity Security and to be mindful of the challenges that come with scaling AI operations. The discussion provided a unique perspective on the experiences of leaders who have successfully governed AI agents at scale, offering valuable lessons for organizations looking to implement AI solutions.
