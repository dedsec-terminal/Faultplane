### Executive Summary
Ukrainian authorities, in cooperation with the FBI, have detained four suspected members of an international ransomware organization. The group is linked to more than $40.5 million in ransom payments from at least 28 companies worldwide. The arrests are part of a broader effort to dismantle the gang’s operations and recover stolen funds.
