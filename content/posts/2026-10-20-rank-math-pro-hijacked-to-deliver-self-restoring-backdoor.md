Copies of Rank Math PRO downloaded from rankmath.com or received as updates between 20:02 UTC Oct 15 and 07:12 UTC Oct 16 were found to contain a self‑restoring backdoor. Sites that installed or activated these versions should be treated as compromised. Rank Math issued a security notice on Oct 16 detailing the issue.
