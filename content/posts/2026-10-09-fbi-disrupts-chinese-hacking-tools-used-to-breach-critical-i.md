### Executive Summary
The FBI seized seven domains that Chinese state-sponsored group Flax Typhoon used to run the MicroScan and FishHub hacking tools. These tools were employed in attacks that compromised critical infrastructure and other organizations worldwide. The seizure disrupts the group’s operations and limits future intrusion capabilities.
