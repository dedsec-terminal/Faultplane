### Executive Summary
On Tuesday, a darknet onion site named 'Luna Moth Files' surfaced, allegedly containing internal chat logs from the Silent Ransom ransomware group. Cybersecurity analysts began probing the leak, but on Thursday the group denied the authenticity of the data, labeling it a hoax. However, evidence from the site suggests the files may be genuine, raising questions about the group's claims and the integrity of the leak.
