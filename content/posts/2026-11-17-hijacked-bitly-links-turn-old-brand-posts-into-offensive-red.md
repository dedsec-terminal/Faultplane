### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs reports that legacy Bitly links embedded in social media posts from brands such as McDonald's and Burger King have been hijacked. When users click the shortened URLs, they are redirected to unrelated Wikipedia pages rather than the intended brand content. The malicious redirects exploit the Bitly infrastructure to serve unintended content, potentially misleading users and undermining brand trust.
