### Executive Summary
A zero-day vulnerability (CVE-2026-754) in Genesis World’s URDF library allows remote attackers to perform directory traversal and disclose sensitive information. Exploitation requires interaction with the library, but attack vectors may vary. The vulnerability has a CVSS score of 5.5.
