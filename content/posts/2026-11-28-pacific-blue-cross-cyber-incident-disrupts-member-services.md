### Executive Summary

Pacific Blue Cross reported a cyber incident that disrupted several of its systems, preventing members from accessing the website and contact centre. The incident was first detected early Wednesday, November, and has affected the provider’s ability to serve its approximately 1.7 million British Columbian members. No further details on the scope or defensive measures were disclosed.
