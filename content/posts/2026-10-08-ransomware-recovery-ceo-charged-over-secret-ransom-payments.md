### Executive Summary
The CEO of MonsterCloud, a ransomware remediation firm, has been charged with fraud for secretly paying attackers to obtain decryptors while claiming to use proprietary technology to recover data. Authorities allege the payments were made covertly and the company misrepresented its recovery methods to victims.
