### Executive Summary
Consumer data broker Radaris.com faces a lawsuit for violating New Jersey privacy law that protects law enforcement officials. The judge ordered Radaris and over a dozen other data broker domains to be transferred to plaintiffs, highlighting the company's history of ignoring removal requests.
