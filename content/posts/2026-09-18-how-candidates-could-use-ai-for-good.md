### Executive Summary
The essay argues that while AI is often seen as a threat to the integrity of the US midterm elections—through voter anxiety, deepfake misinformation, and propaganda—candidates also have a chance to harness AI to improve campaigning. By using AI to analyze voter concerns, engage constituents inclusively, and craft responsive policy platforms, candidates could make elections more responsive and democratic.
