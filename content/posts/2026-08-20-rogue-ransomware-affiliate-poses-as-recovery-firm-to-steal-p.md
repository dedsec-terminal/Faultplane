### Executive Summary
A suspected ransomware affiliate is masquerading as a recovery service named "Ransom Busters", contacting victims before their attacks become public. It claims to provide decryption keys and data deletion for a fee, but actually steals payments from victims. The scheme exploits trust in legitimate recovery firms to defraud users.
