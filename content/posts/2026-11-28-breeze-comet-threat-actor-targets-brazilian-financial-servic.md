### Executive Summary

Mandiant investigated a series of compromises in 2024 that affected Brazilian financial services, retail, and e‑commerce organizations. Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) tracks the actor, BREEZE COMET (formerly UNC5669), as a financially motivated threat group that manipulates payment systems such as Pix, STR, and Boleto to conduct fraudulent transfers. The group uses a customized malware suite, compromised trusted government and municipal websites, and social engineering—including voice phishing and remote monitoring tools—to gain initial access and maintain persistence in target Active Directory and cloud environments. Evidence suggests BREEZE COMET is leveraging generative AI to accelerate malware development, and it may expand its infrastructure footprint to other Latin American and African countries. GTIG and Mandiant have issued mitigation recommendations and detection guidance to help organizations defend against this evolving threat.
