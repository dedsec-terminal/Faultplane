### Executive Summary
Nikkei Inc. announced a new data breach, its third in roughly a month, exposing 1.83 million personal information records. The breach involved sensitive data such as names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and other personal identifiers. The company confirmed the incident and is investigating the scope and impact of the exposure.
