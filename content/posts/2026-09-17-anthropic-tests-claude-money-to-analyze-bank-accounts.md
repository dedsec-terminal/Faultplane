### Executive Summary
Anthropic is piloting a new personal finance feature called Claude Money that lets users link bank accounts to the AI assistant Claude. The feature aims to provide insights into spending and budgeting by analyzing transaction data. While the integration promises convenience, it raises privacy and security concerns about how financial data is handled and stored.
