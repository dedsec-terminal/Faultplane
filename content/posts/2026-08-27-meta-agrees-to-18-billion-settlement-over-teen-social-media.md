### Executive Summary
Meta has agreed to a proposed settlement of up to $18 billion with a bipartisan coalition of 52 U.S. attorneys general. The settlement addresses allegations that Facebook and Instagram were intentionally designed to encourage compulsive use among children and teenagers, potentially violating consumer protection laws. The deal aims to compensate affected users and implement safeguards to reduce harmful content exposure.
