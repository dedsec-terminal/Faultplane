### Executive Summary
Microsoft released patches for record 973 vulnerabilities, 113 critical, largest Patch Tuesday to date. Two vulnerabilities were exploited in the wild; none were publicly disclosed before the release. Key fixes include Windows privilege escalation and critical remote code execution flaws in Skype for Business, MSMQ, and RRAS.

**Original Description:**
This month, Microsoft released patches for a record-breaking 973 vulnerabilities, including 113 rated critical. It is by far the largest Patch Tuesday to date, well ahead of the previous high of 664 set in July 2026. Two vulnerabilities are listed as exploited in the wild, while none were publicly disclosed before Patch Tuesday. Notable fixes include Windows privilege escalation and critical RCEs in Skype for Business, MSMQ and RRAS.&#xd;
