### Executive Summary
More than 70 fake crypto sites lure users with promises of extra rewards for voting, then prompt them to connect wallets, potentially exposing funds. The sites mimic legitimate platforms like xStocks and Pendle, using deceptive voting mechanisms to harvest wallet addresses and facilitate theft.
