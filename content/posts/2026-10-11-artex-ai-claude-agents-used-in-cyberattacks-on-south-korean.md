### Executive Summary
Earlier this month, a Chinese hacker launched a series of cyberattacks against South Korean banks using the ARTEX AI penetration testing suite and Claude AI agents. The attacks exploited automated reconnaissance and exploitation capabilities, allowing the attacker to compromise multiple banking systems and potentially exfiltrate sensitive data. The incidents highlighted the growing threat of AI‑powered tools in the financial sector.
