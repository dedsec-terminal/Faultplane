### Executive Summary
TerminalFix mimics the familiar ClickFix fake CAPTCHA trick to lure users into downloading a malicious payload. Unlike typical ClickFix malware, this variant can grant attackers lateral movement within the victim’s network, enabling broader access and potential data exfiltration.
