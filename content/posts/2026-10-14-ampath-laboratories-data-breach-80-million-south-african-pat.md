### Executive Summary
Ampath Laboratories, a major South African pathology provider, was hit by a cyberattack that led to the exfiltration of over 80 million patient records. The threat actor ByteToBreach, known for attacks across Africa, claimed responsibility. The breach exposes sensitive health data and raises concerns about data protection in the region.
