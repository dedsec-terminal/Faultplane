### Executive Summary
Cisco Talos has identified eight intermediate certificates issued by the eMudhra certificate authority that have been compromised. The discovery indicates potential misuse of these certificates for malicious activities, raising concerns about the integrity of eMudhra’s intermediate CA infrastructure. Talos is monitoring the situation and advising stakeholders to verify certificate chains and revoke affected certificates.
