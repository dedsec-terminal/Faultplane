### Executive Summary
Apple has backported a fix for a CoreGraphics vulnerability that was reportedly exploited in targeted attacks. The update now covers iPhone XS, XS Max, XR, and the seventh‑generation iPad. Users of these older devices should install the latest iOS update to mitigate the risk.
