### Executive Summary
Cursor confirmed a security breach that allowed attackers to distribute a malicious Claude Code extension via its marketplace. Hackers stole credentials from AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI, enabling them to upload the rogue extension. The incident highlights risks of third‑party extensions and credential compromise in AI ecosystems.
