### Executive Summary
Varonis Threat Labs disclosed three vulnerabilities in Microsoft Copilot Personal, dubbed CoSnitch, that enable a single click on a crafted link to silently pull data from connected apps and other information available to the victim's Copilot session. The flaws exploit an undocumented URL parameter surfaced by the assistant itself.
