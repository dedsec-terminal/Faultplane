### Executive Summary
The essay, co‑authored with Cindy Cohn and published in Lawfare, traces how the 9/11 attacks spurred a shift from targeted wiretaps to mass surveillance of internet and telephone metadata. It argues that the legal and technical framework created for counter‑terrorism has expanded beyond its original purpose, becoming a routine tool for law enforcement agencies such as ICE. The authors warn that the unchecked growth of mass surveillance threatens civil liberties and undermines the rule of law.
