### Executive Summary
Leaked documents reveal a force‑generation mechanism for several Russian General Staff components, including the GRU, Main Operational Directorate, and 8th Directorate, detailing training in protected communications, cryptography, and information security. The leak also links a 2024 Department No.4 graduate, Aleksei Kondrashov, to Military Unit 74455 (Sandworm), a unit known for destructive cyber operations such as the 2017 NotPetya attack. The documents do not confirm that every listed graduate participated in named operations, only that they were assigned to the unit.
