### Executive Summary
The webinar explores how identity blind spots can expose security operations centers (SOCs) to risk, highlighting challenges in attributing suspicious activity to users, machine accounts, or AI agents. It covers the impact of these blind spots on alert fatigue and incident response, and presents strategies and tools for detecting, monitoring, and closing gaps in identity visibility to strengthen SOC defenses.
