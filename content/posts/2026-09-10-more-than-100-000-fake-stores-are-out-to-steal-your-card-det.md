### Executive Summary
DoppelCart is a malicious operation that creates thousands of counterfeit online storefronts mimicking legitimate retailers. These fake sites lure shoppers into entering payment information and one-time bank confirmation codes, which the attackers harvest for fraudulent transactions. The campaign has reportedly deployed over 100,000 such sites, posing a significant threat to consumers and merchants.
