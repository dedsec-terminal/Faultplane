### Executive Summary
Trail of Bits demonstrates how Codex accelerates backporting security patches to stable Linux releases. By automating patch adaptation, validation, and submission, the workflow cuts human effort from days to hours, enabling 149 CVE fixes in the kernel and updates for Fedora and Ubuntu LTS by Oct 29. The approach reduces backlog and makes neglected security work practical.
