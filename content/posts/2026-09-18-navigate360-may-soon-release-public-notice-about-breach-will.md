### Executive Summary
A hacktivist disclosed that it accessed 8.3 million tips submitted through anonymous tip lines and platforms used by schools, communities, Crime Stoppers, law enforcement and the military. Six months after the breach, no affected individuals have been notified. DataBreaches.net reports that Navigate360 has not provided public transparency or a notification plan.
