### Executive Summary
OpenAI announced that ChatGPT is experiencing a widespread outage, preventing users from logging in, creating new accounts, and accessing existing conversations. The service disruption began earlier this week and has affected both web and mobile platforms. OpenAI is working to restore functionality and has urged users to check status updates for further information.
