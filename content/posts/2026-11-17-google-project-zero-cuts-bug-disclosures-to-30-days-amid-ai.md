### Executive Summary
Google’s Project Zero is shortening its vulnerability disclosure window from a 90‑day “90+30” policy to a fixed 30‑day deadline. The change aims to reduce the window for AI‑driven vulnerability discovery and exploitation, ensuring faster patching and mitigating emerging AI‑powered threats.
