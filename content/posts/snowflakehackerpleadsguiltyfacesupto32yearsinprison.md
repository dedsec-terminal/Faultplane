A 26-year-old Canadian man, Connor Riley Moucka, has pleaded guilty to hacking into customer accounts at cloud storage provider Snowflake and stealing data from over 165 organizations. Moucka, also known as "Waifu" and "Judische," faces up to 32 years in prison for his crimes, which include computer fraud, wire fraud, aggravated identity theft, and conspiracy. He is set to be sentenced on October 27.

Between April and September 2024, Moucka and his co-conspirators used stolen credentials to access Snowflake customer accounts, downloading terabytes of sensitive data, including financial information, Social Security numbers, and other personally identifiable information. They then demanded ransom payments in exchange for not publishing or selling the stolen data, receiving over $2.5 million in payments. Moucka personally received at least $495,000 from the scheme, which also involved advertising stolen data for sale on various online platforms.

The Justice Department estimates that the victim companies suffered over $9.5 million in actual losses, with additional losses suffered by the companies' customers totaling at least 100 million individuals. Organizations affected by the Snowflake campaign include AT&T, Ticketmaster, and Neiman Marcus, among others. Law enforcement officials have condemned Moucka's actions, stating that his threats and re-extortion tactics were "calculated and predatory" and caused real harm to his victims. The FBI has emphasized that Moucka's guilty plea demonstrates that "hiding behind a screen is no shield from justice."
