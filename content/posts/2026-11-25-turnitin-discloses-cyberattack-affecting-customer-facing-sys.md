### Executive Summary
Turnitin announced that it detected unauthorized activity on some customer-facing systems. The company said the intrusion was identified through monitoring and that no customer data was compromised. The incident was reported by CEO Chris Caren in a statement. Turnitin is investigating the breach and has taken steps to secure affected systems.
