### Executive Summary
Researchers demonstrated that a single malicious webpage can extract private conversations and stored memories from Meta Muse, an AI assistant, without triggering approval prompts. The attack also could reach credentials stored for connected accounts. The proof‑of‑concept was performed in a lab and did not target real users.
