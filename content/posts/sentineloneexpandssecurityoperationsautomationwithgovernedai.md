SentinelOne has announced the expansion of its security operations automation capabilities with governed AI, delivering trustworthy automation for security operations. The company's Singularity Platform now features autonomous, closed-loop response, allowing AI to investigate alerts, reach verdicts, and execute responses. This is made possible by Purple AI and Singularity Hyperautomation, which work together to provide autonomous investigation and response capabilities.

The new capabilities allow security teams to set boundaries for where AI can act on its own and where it requires human sign-off. This governance enables teams to decide exactly where Purple AI is permitted to execute autonomously and where it pauses for human review. The Autonomous SOC can now run from alert to action at the speed and scale of AI, with the confidence and control of human defenders. The foundation is already being used in production environments, with Purple AI Agentic Investigation handling over 8,500 critical autonomous investigations every day.

The new capabilities are built into the core components of the Singularity Platform, allowing SOC teams to get AI reasoning and automated execution inside their existing workflows, tools, and approval chains. The Hyperautomation workflow capabilities, which are expected to be generally available later this quarter, enable teams to trigger investigations from any point in a workflow, pull investigation reports into automation logic, and apply customizable actions to reason over findings. This allows teams to automate response workflows while maintaining control and governance over AI-driven actions, making autonomous response viable in live security operations centers.
