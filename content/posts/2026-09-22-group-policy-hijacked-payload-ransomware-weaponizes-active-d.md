### Executive Summary
Kaspersky GERT experts dissect PAYLOAD ransomware, an encryption‑less, binary‑less threat that hijacks Active Directory Group Policy Objects (GPOs) to propagate and execute malicious payloads. The analysis details how PAYLOAD leverages AD mechanisms for lateral movement and persistence, bypassing traditional security controls and highlighting the need for hardened GPO management.
