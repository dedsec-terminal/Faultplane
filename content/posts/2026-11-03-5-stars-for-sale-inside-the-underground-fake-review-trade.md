### Executive Summary
Underground sellers offer to provide five‑star reviews for up to $1,100, and can also remove negative feedback. The scheme targets online businesses, exploiting review platforms to boost ratings and hide criticism. The operation involves a marketplace where buyers pay for fabricated positive reviews and deletion services, undermining trust in e‑commerce reputations.
