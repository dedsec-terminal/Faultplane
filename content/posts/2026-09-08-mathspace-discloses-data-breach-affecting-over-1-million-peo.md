### Executive Summary
Mathspace, an online maths learning platform, announced a data breach that exposed personal information of more than 1 million students, staff, and parents. Attackers accessed the platform’s Metabase internal reporting system, compromising usernames, email addresses, and other sensitive data. The breach was discovered over the weekend and the company is investigating the extent of the compromise.
