An investigation into the CareCloud data breach, originally estimated to affect about 350,000 people, now indicates that the incident impacted approximately 3.7 million individuals, according to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services breach tracker. The breach exposed personal health information of patients across multiple healthcare providers.
