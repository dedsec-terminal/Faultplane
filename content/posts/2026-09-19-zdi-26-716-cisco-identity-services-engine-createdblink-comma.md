### Executive Summary
A command injection flaw in Cisco Identity Services Engine’s createDBLink function allows authenticated attackers to execute arbitrary code remotely. The vulnerability, identified as CVE-2026-20176, carries a CVSS score of 7.2 and was disclosed by the Zero Day Initiative.
