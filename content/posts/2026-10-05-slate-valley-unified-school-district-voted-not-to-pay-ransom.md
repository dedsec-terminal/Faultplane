### Executive Summary
The Slate Valley Unified School District in Fair Haven, Vermont, declined to pay a ransom demanded by the Kairos threat actors. The district reported no evidence of student data compromise, but Kairos threatened to leak data. The incident began on September 3, and Kairos contacted DataBreaches on October 2 to inform them of the situation.
