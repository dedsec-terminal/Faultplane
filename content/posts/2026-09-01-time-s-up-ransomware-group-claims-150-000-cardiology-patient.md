### Executive Summary
On Aug 6, DataBreaches reported that Cardiology Associates of Port Huron (CAPH), a Michigan medical practice with 9 locations, was breached by the ransomware group Orova. The group claimed to have accessed over 150,000 cardiology patient records, including screenshots of personally identifiable and protected health information, posted on its leak site on Aug 4.
