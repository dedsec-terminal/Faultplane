### Executive Summary
GuidePoint Research and Intelligence Team (GRIT) identified a new phishing trend where attackers pose as a third‑party service called "Ransom Busters" and email victims offering to help recover from ransomware. The emails claim to provide decryption tools or assistance, but are actually a lure to gain access to victim systems. GRIT advises users to verify any unsolicited recovery offers and to rely on official vendor support or reputable security partners.
