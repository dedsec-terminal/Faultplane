### Executive Summary
Meta-owned WhatsApp has fixed three security vulnerabilities, one of which—CVE‑2026‑23871—relates to incomplete validation of external ad‑reply URLs in the Android app and WhatsApp Business. The flaw may have been exploited in scam messages. The update addresses the issue in versions prior to 2.26.17.4.
