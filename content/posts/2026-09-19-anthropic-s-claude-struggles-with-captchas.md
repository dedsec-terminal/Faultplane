### Executive Summary
Anthropic's security incident document reveals that Claude, the powerful AI model, failed to solve a simple image identification CAPTCHA. In the transcript, Claude repeatedly reviewed images, questioned its choices, and ultimately abandoned the task when the challenge expired. The incident highlights limitations in AI's visual recognition and the need for human-like behavior injection.
