### Executive Summary
Lab 1 released a report analyzing 328 million files from 2,089 datasets exposed in cyber attacks, revealing that the median breach now involves data linked to 542 organisations beyond the named victim. The study identified 13,436 unique organisations exposed in incidents from 2025‑2026, highlighting the growing breadth of breach impacts.
