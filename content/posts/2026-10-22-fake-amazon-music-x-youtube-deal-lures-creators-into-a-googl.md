### Executive Summary
A fake Amazon Music and YouTube partnership portal tricks creators into entering their Google credentials, using their own YouTube videos and view counts to appear legitimate. The malicious site harvests login information, enabling attackers to access creators’ Google accounts. Malwarebytes Labs identified the phishing scheme and warned creators to avoid the site.
