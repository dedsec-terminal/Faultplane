### Executive Summary
AnonyMousKIT is a newly uncovered phishing‑as‑a‑service platform that automates the collection of iPhone passcodes via voice AI agents. Attackers use the stolen passcodes to unlock stolen Apple devices and bypass the Activation Lock, enabling resale or further exploitation. The service offers a turnkey solution for criminals, including pre‑built scripts, voice synthesis, and a marketplace for stolen credentials.
