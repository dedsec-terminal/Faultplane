### Executive Summary
Microsoft Defender Research and the AI Red Team reveal how attackers can poison training data or model checkpoints to hijack coding agents. By inserting malicious code into datasets or checkpoints, adversaries can trigger remote code execution and other harmful actions when the agent is used. The study highlights the risk of supply‑chain attacks on AI models and the need for robust safeguards.
