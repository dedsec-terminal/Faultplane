### Executive Summary
U.S. intelligence agencies have issued a low‑confidence assessment that Iran was behind the fridge‑related cyberattacks in France, according to three anonymous officials. The assessment links the incidents to Iran broadly, not a specific group, and suggests Iran may also be responsible for similar attacks across Europe, though investigations are in early stages.
