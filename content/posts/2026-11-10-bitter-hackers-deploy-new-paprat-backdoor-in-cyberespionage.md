### Executive Summary
The South Asian cyber‑espionage group Bitter (also known as APT‑C‑08, APT‑Q‑37, Hazy Tiger, Orange Yali, T‑APT‑17, and TA397) has been observed deploying a previously unreported remote access trojan, PapRAT, against organizations in the region. PapRAT is available in both C++ and Rust implementations and offers capabilities such as command execution and file manipulation, enabling the group to conduct covert data exfiltration and system control. The operation demonstrates the group’s continued focus on high‑value targets in South Asia.
