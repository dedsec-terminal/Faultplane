### Executive Summary
A suspected supply chain attack on Optimizely injected malicious JavaScript into the Optimizely CDN, infecting high‑profile sites such as CNN, Fox News, BBC, Salesforce, Zoom, and GitLab. The injected scripts steal cryptocurrency and payment data and exploit iOS vulnerabilities, exposing visitors to theft and exploitation.
