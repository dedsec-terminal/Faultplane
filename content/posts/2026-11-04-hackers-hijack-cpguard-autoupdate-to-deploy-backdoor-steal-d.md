### Executive Summary
Attackers compromised the cPGuard web security suite’s update mechanism, injecting a backdoor and a data‑stealing script into legitimate updates. The hijacked autoupdate channel allowed the malware to silently install on affected systems, exfiltrating sensitive information while remaining hidden behind trusted software updates.
