### Executive Summary

When discussing IT infrastructure security, the focus is usually on firewalls, EDR, backup, vulnerability management, and network segmentation. However, all of them are effective only under one condition — if the infrastructure itself is under control. Incidents often begin not with the exploitation of a vulnerability or bypassing protection systems, but with shortcomings in basic infrastructure management. Every virtual machine, server, access, and IP address must have an owner, a lifetime, and a controlled path for creation and deletion. When management processes are opaque, the infrastructure itself becomes a source of information security risks, and security tools have to operate over an unknown environment and merely contain the consequences.
