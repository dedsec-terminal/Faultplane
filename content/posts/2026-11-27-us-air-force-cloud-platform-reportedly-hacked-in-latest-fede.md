### Executive Summary

Hackers have reportedly breached the US Air Force's Cloud One computing platform, adding to a recent wave of cyberattacks affecting US government agencies, including the FBI, CIA, and Treasury Department. According to Reuters , the intrusion was discovered within the past month during an upgrade of the cloud platform, disrupting application migrations and software updates. Launched in 2017, Cloud One is an enterprise cloud computing platform managed by the US Air Force that allows Department of Defense agencies to host and manage applications supporting military operations, personnel management, logistics, and maintenance.
