### Executive Summary

The article reports that threat actors are increasingly employing open‑source, agentic pentesting frameworks—such as ARTEX, Hexstrike, CAIRN, and PentAGI—to automate intrusions at machine speed. Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) have observed these tools in incident‑response engagements and research telemetry since late August 2026, noting that they enable autonomous lateral movement, database exploitation, and exfiltration preparation. The observed impact is a dramatic acceleration and scaling of attack activity, with agents autonomously exploring multiple attack paths and troubleshooting errors in seconds or minutes. Defensive guidance highlighted includes control‑plane isolation, short‑lived credentials, least privilege, segmentation, and behavioral detection—long‑standing best practices that must be re‑examined to counter the new autonomous threat landscape.
