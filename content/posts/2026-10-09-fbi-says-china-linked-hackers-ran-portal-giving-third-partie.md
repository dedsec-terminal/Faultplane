### Executive Summary
On Oct. 8, the FBI announced that hackers linked to the Chinese cybersecurity firm Integrity Technology Group operated a portal that allowed third parties to access stolen emails from government agencies, law enforcement, healthcare providers, and religious organizations across Southeast Asia. The U.S. and U.K. have sanctioned the firm, and investigators say the attackers used a scanning tool to find website vulnerabilities.
