### Executive Summary
Russian authorities detained three suspected members of the DataSuckers hacking group after a cyberattack on a popular restaurant chain that exposed customer data. A Moscow court placed 19-year-old Denis Fridman under house arrest until December 8 in connection with the Dodo Pizza breach.
