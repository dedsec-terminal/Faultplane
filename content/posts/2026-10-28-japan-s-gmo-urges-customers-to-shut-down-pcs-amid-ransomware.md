### Executive Summary
The GMO Group, a Japanese hosting provider, warned customers to disconnect from the internet and shut down PCs after a threat actor breached its Onamae Desktop Cloud service and threatened a ransomware attack. The alert urged users to take immediate precautions to prevent potential data loss and system compromise.
