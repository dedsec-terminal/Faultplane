### Executive Summary
Hackers drained about 4,000 BTC (≈$320 M) from the federation wallet backing Blockstream’s Liquid sidechain. The attackers claimed to be “good guys,” but the incident exposed a vulnerability in Liquid’s wallet management, affecting exchanges and other financial institutions that use the network.
