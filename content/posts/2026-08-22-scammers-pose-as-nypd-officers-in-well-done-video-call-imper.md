### Executive Summary
Scammers impersonated NYPD officers and posed as American Express representatives in a video‑call scam. They claimed the victim, Eddie, had a name linked to a firearm purchase attempt and that AmEx suspected identity theft. The victim, who had no AmEx account, was initially unaware of the fraud. The incident highlights the use of law‑enforcement impersonation to deceive individuals into revealing personal information.
