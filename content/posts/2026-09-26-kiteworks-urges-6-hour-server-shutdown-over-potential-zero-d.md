### Executive Summary
Secure file‑sharing vendor Kiteworks has advised all customers to shut down their servers for a six‑hour window on Saturday after receiving threat‑intelligence that a zero‑day exploit could be used against its platform. The advisory, issued via email and the company’s website, warns of a potential imminent cyberattack and recommends disabling services to mitigate risk. Kiteworks is monitoring the situation and will provide updates as more information becomes available.
