### Executive Summary
Researchers at Hacktron used Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5 to chain two vulnerabilities: a bug in OpenAI’s public help‑forum software and a weakness in OpenAI’s login system. The exploit allowed them to hijack ChatGPT and Codex accounts of several OpenAI employees and access an internal code repository. The incident demonstrates how AI can be leveraged to orchestrate multi‑stage attacks on high‑profile targets.
