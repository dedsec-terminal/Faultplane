### Executive Summary
Over 9,300 AWS access keys were publicly exposed from August 2022 to August 2026 and remain active. The leaked credentials grant full administrative control over corporate AWS accounts, enabling attackers to manipulate resources, exfiltrate data, and deploy malicious workloads. The exposure highlights ongoing credential management gaps and the need for stricter key rotation and monitoring.
