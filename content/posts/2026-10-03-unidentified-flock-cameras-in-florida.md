### Executive Summary
St. Lucie County, Florida, discovered a dozen Flock surveillance cameras on county property whose owners could not be identified and that had not been authorized by county officials. The incident echoes a decade‑old case in Washington, DC, where StingRay cell‑phone surveillance devices were operated by unknown parties, raising concerns about unapproved surveillance equipment and potential privacy violations.
