### Executive Summary
Hackers are exploiting fake event invitations from OpenAI, SpaceX, and Anthropic to lure employees into phishing attacks. They employ two distinct phishing-as-a-service platforms to harvest Microsoft and Google account credentials and authentication sessions, enabling credential theft and potential lateral movement within corporate environments.
