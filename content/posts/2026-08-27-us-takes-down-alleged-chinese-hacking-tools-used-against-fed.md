### Executive Summary
The U.S. Department of Justice announced the takedown of two Chinese hacking platforms, QScan and QTRouter, used by China’s Ministry of State Security to breach federal agencies including the Federal Reserve, DOJ and Senate since 2018. The tools were operated by Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology Company. The takedown disrupts ongoing espionage operations.
