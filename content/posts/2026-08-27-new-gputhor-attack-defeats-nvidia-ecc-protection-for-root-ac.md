### Executive Summary
A newly disclosed Rowhammer attack, GPUThor, can bypass error‑correcting code (ECC) protections on NVIDIA GPUs. By repeatedly accessing memory, the exploit induces bit flips that allow attackers to trigger a denial‑of‑service and gain root‑level privilege escalation on affected systems.
