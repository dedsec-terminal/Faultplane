### Executive Summary
The article reviews security vendors showcased at Black Hat, noting the pervasive influence of AI across identity, SaaS, application security, and data spaces. It highlights a market split into three tool types: assessment (diagnostics), defense (adversary blocking), and prevention (proactive protection). Despite expectations, diagnostic tools dominate the scene, leaving many unresolved problems amplified by AI.
