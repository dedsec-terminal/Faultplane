### Executive Summary
A malware campaign uses fake software‑download sites that impersonate trusted vendors to deliver malicious installers. The installers disable Windows Update and weaken Microsoft Defender, compromising users seeking popular software. The attack has affected multiple organizations, especially China‑based operations of multinational companies and Chinese‑speaking users.
