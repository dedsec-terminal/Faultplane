### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers revealed NovaCookies, an adversary‑in‑the‑middle phishing toolkit that masquerades as legitimate DocuSign notifications to lure users into Microsoft 365 sign‑ins. The tool acts as a proxy, redirecting authentication traffic and capturing active sessions, enabling attackers to hijack accounts. The service is subscription‑based at $320/month and is sold to threat actors.
