### Executive Summary
Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) confirmed that personal employee data was leaked online after a threat actor uploaded 6.9 GB of information allegedly stolen from the company to an auction site. The breach appears linked to a cloud provider used by TSMC, raising concerns about third‑party data security. The leaked data includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, and employment details of thousands of employees. TSMC has not yet disclosed the full extent of the compromise or the identity of the attacker.
