### Executive Summary
Microsoft Security Blog investigated the ESC1 and ESC8 intrusion paths. Attackers leveraged Teams support impersonation to gain remote access, execute malicious runtime, and abuse Active Directory Certificate Services via authentication relay and Kerberos. The post details detection, mitigation, and response steps.
