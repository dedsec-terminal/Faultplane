### Executive Summary
Microsoft’s Digital Crimes Unit (DCU) exposed a Chinese-language cybercriminal ecosystem, linking phishing kit operators (Storm‑2990) and an illicit marketplace (Storm‑3119) that sold phishing tools and infrastructure. Working with global law‑enforcement partners, Microsoft disrupted the network, shutting down key components of the phishing infrastructure.
