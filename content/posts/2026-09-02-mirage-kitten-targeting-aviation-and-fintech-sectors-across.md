### Executive Summary
Kaspersky researchers uncovered Mirage Kitten attacks that target aviation and FinTech companies in the Middle East and Africa. The campaign uses two previously undocumented malware families: NodeRabbit, written in Node.js, and PollCat, a JavaScript-based tool. These tools expand Mirage Kitten’s threat surface and demonstrate its evolving capabilities.
