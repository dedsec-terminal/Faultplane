### Executive Summary
U.S. officials have retracted earlier statements that several federal agencies—including the Senate, Federal Reserve, and NASA—were hacked by Chinese spies, now saying those agencies were among the targets of the alleged attacks. The Justice Department revised its statement, indicating the agencies were targeted but not necessarily compromised.
