### Executive Summary
Security researchers identified a new data‑theft and extortion group called Inception, which they believe is a rebrand of the ShinyHunters gang. In a statement, Inception rejects the attribution, asserting it has no affiliation with ShinyHunters or any other known threat actor. The group continues to target victims for data theft and extortion, but its leadership insists it operates independently.
