### Executive Summary
The Ninth Circuit ruled that a breach of confidential health information gives affected plan participants standing to sue for damages, rejecting the risk‑based approach used in other circuits. The decision departs from the precedent set by TransUnion and emphasizes the duty of health plans to protect patient data. The ruling could broaden liability for health insurers and providers in data‑breach litigation.
