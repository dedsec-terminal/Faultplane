### Executive Summary
A fraudulent Prime Gaming promotion targeted FIFA 27 players, directing them to a counterfeit reward site that captured EA account passwords and two‑factor authentication codes. The malicious site mimicked official branding, tricking users into entering credentials that were then exfiltrated to attackers. The incident highlights the risk of phishing campaigns that combine gaming incentives with credential harvesting.
