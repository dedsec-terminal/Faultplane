### Executive Summary
Truffle Security has launched a free Secrets Revocation Program that identifies and helps revoke 1.4 million publicly exposed API keys and other secrets that remain valid. The initiative scans public repositories, cloud storage, and other sources to locate exposed credentials, then provides issuers with a streamlined process to revoke them, reducing the risk of unauthorized access.
