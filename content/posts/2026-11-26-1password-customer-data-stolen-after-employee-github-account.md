### Executive Summary
1Password announced a data breach after an employee’s corporate GitHub account was compromised. Attackers used the account to deploy malicious code into 1Password’s backend services, enabling them to exfiltrate customer data. The breach highlights risks of third‑party access and the importance of securing developer accounts.
