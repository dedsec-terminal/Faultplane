### Executive Summary
IDC Frontier Inc., a SoftBank subsidiary that provides cloud services, was hit by a ransomware attack on Oct. 7, causing a system outage that affected 495 corporate and local government customers. The company is investigating the extent of the damage and whether any personal information was compromised.
