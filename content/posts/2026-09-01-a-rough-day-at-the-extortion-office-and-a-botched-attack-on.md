### Executive Summary
DataBreaches.net received a tip about a Click2Mail incident and a separate email from a patient claiming that an extortionist compromised Blossom Health’s platform or a provider’s account (Justine Belesario) and sent a ransom demand. The report indicates a botched attack on the telehealth and psychiatry service, potentially exposing patient data.
