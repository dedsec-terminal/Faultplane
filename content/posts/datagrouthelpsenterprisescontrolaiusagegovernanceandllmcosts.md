Here is a 3-paragraph summary of the article:

SelectHub has launched DataGrout, a platform designed to help enterprises control AI usage, governance, and Large Language Model (LLM) costs. DataGrout aims to reduce token waste and provide IT and FinOps leadership with a policy-driven, auditable system to track company-wide AI utilization. This is particularly important as enterprises increase their use of LLMs, which can lead to significant strain on corporate budgets due to uncontrolled token waste and hidden API expenses.

DataGrout addresses this challenge through a unified connection layer and a symbolic inference layer that reduces calls to the underlying LLM. Early tests have shown promising results, with token reduction averaging 60% for data-intensive tasks. The platform provides precise token tracking and visibility into LLM consumption patterns, enabling leadership to measure the value delivered by AI investments and eliminate waste. This is a critical need for enterprises, as many are struggling to assess the extent to which their LLM resources are driving business value versus being wasted on unnecessary or unauthorized projects.

Industry leaders have echoed the need for tools like DataGrout, citing the twin challenges of token economics and governance. DataGrout's platform acts as a gateway and control plane, allowing agents to connect via its Conduit SDK or other protocols. It can be connected to LLMs directly or via popular AI gateways, and offers its own MCP servers for popular SaaS applications. By reducing token usage and increasing transparency, DataGrout aims to help enterprises reduce waste, prevent abuse, and forecast ROI more accurately, ultimately driving more effective and efficient use of AI resources.
