### Executive Summary
ASOS customers received a push notification claiming a breach of the company. The message was sent by attackers and appears to be a phishing attempt designed to trick users into revealing credentials or installing malware. The incident demonstrates how push notifications can be weaponized for social engineering. No evidence of data exfiltration or malware delivery has been reported yet.
