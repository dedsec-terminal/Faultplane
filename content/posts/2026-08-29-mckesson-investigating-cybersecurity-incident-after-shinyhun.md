### Executive Summary
McKesson, a major U.S. healthcare and pharmaceutical distributor, disclosed a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to third‑party applications and the theft of 284 million patient records. The extortion group ShinyHunters claims responsibility, demanding ransom. McKesson is investigating the breach and assessing impact.
