### Executive Summary
Australian Federal Police (AFP) arrested two men, aged 21 and 23, from Western Australia suspected of belonging to the cybercrime group TeamPCP, known for the longest-running software supply‑chain attack spree. AFP released a statement but did not name the suspects; KrebsOnSecurity has identified the 21‑year‑old suspect. The article includes interviews with TeamPCP’s spokesperson and analysis of clues that led to the arrests.
