### Executive Summary
Lookalike Amazon sites advertise high‑paying remote jobs, but the offers are actually reshipping scams. The sites lure applicants with $5,000/month promises, then require upfront fees or personal data. Malwarebytes Labs warns that these fake postings are designed to steal money and credentials, not legitimate employment.
