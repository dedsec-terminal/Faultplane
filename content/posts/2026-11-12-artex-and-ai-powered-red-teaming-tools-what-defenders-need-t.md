### Executive Summary
Unit 42 uncovered that adversaries are leveraging AI-driven red teaming tools, such as ARTEX, to automate reconnaissance, vulnerability exploitation, and post-exploitation tasks. The report details how these tools can generate realistic attack scenarios, evade detection, and accelerate attack timelines. Defenders are advised to monitor for anomalous AI-generated activity, harden detection capabilities, and adopt defensive AI to counteract these evolving threats.
