ShinyHunters, a high-profile hacking group, claims to have breached FBI-related services, including the FBI job applicants’ site, and stolen personal data of FBI employees and applicants—names, addresses, phone numbers, and spouse information. The group alleges the breach could be massive, but the FBI has not confirmed the incident.
