### Executive Summary
Justin.sh reports a leaked Cellebrite document describing a new product, Advanced Unlocks (AU), slated for 2026. AU is a next‑generation AFU vector designed to be more resilient to Apple OS changes, works with Stolen Device Protection (tightening USB restrictions), and is enabled by a dedicated appliance with an approximate 8‑meter range. The leak raises concerns about a potential iPhone radio zero‑day vulnerability.
