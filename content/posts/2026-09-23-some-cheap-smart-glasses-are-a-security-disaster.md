### Executive Summary
Tests by Malwarebytes Labs revealed that inexpensive smart glasses can be hijacked via Bluetooth, allowing attackers to access and exfiltrate users’ photos, videos, and other personal data. The vulnerability stems from weak authentication and open Bluetooth channels, making the devices an attractive target for data theft.
