### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs reports a phone return scam where fraudsters deliver a legitimate phone, then fake a return request, causing the victim to be billed for the return. The scheme relies on convincing social engineering and exploits billing systems, leaving victims with unexpected charges.
