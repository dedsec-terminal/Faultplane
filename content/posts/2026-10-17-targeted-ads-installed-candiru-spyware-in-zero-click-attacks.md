### Executive Summary
Candiru spyware was delivered to Al Jazeera Media Network devices through a zero‑click attack that leveraged malicious online advertisements. The malware was installed simply by rendering the ads on the victim’s device, requiring no user interaction. The attack demonstrates how targeted ad campaigns can serve as a vector for sophisticated spyware, compromising corporate networks without traditional phishing or exploit steps.
