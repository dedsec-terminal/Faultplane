### Executive Summary
Securelist reports a new iteration of the MacSync malware, a macOS stealer that now includes a backdoor module aimed at crypto enthusiasts and developers. The update introduces novel delivery mechanisms and a redesigned payload, enhancing persistence and data exfiltration capabilities. Analysts note the threat’s focus on cryptocurrency wallets and development tools, expanding its target set beyond typical financial victims.
