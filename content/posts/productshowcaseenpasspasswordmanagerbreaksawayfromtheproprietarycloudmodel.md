Enpass is a password manager that allows users to store sensitive information, such as passwords, payment cards, and identities, in encrypted vaults. Unlike other password managers, Enpass does not rely on a proprietary cloud model, instead giving users the option to store their vaults on their device or in a cloud storage service of their choice, including iCloud, Google Drive, and Dropbox. This flexibility makes Enpass a great option for users who value control over their data.

The Enpass app is available on multiple platforms, including Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS, and offers browser extensions for several popular browsers. The app's interface is user-friendly, with a focus on security recommendations and a Security Score that summarizes password health. Enpass also includes a range of features, such as a built-in password generator, AutoFill, and breach monitoring, to help users manage their passwords and stay secure. Additionally, the app supports unlimited vaults, custom categories, and tags, making it easy to organize and separate work and personal credentials.

Enpass is a great choice for users who want a flexible and feature-rich password manager that doesn't rely on a proprietary cloud model. With its broad platform support, flexible storage options, and extensive set of item templates, Enpass is well-suited for users who work across multiple devices and platforms. The app's ability to import data from other password managers and its support for passkeys, TOTP, and other advanced security features make it a strong contender in the password management market. Overall, Enpass offers a unique combination of flexibility, security, and features that make it an attractive option for users looking for a password manager that meets their needs.
