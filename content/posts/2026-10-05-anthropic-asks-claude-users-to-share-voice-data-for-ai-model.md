### Executive Summary
Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, has begun requesting that users of its Claude chatbot voluntarily share voice recordings of their conversations. The company says the data will be used to fine‑tune and improve Claude’s language and speech models. Users can opt‑in through the app, and Anthropic will anonymize the recordings before training. The move raises privacy concerns about how voice data is collected, stored, and used for AI development.
