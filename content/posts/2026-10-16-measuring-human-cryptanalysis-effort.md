### Executive Summary
Matthew Green’s study estimates the human effort spent on public‑key cryptanalysis by analyzing ~16,000 papers. It classifies work on ECDLP, lattice‑based, and syndrome‑decoding attacks, converting publications into research‑allocation years via author mapping. Findings show lattice research has recently surpassed elliptic‑curve work in cumulative effort. The analysis was performed using Claude 5.1/5.5 and GPT‑6 Astra, with Green guiding methodology.
