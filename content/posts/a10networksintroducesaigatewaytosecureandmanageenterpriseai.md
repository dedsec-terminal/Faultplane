A10 Networks has introduced the A10 AI Gateway, a centralized control plane that enables organizations to secure and manage enterprise AI. The gateway provides unified routing, cost management, and governance across all AI agents, applications, and large language models used within an organization. This is particularly important as AI adoption accelerates, and developers increasingly build AI into applications and business systems, making it challenging for teams to have visibility into AI usage, performance, and costs.

The A10 AI Gateway addresses these challenges by bringing all AI interactions into a single, governed environment. It allows organizations to optimize performance, control spend, centralize observability, enforce security, and provide developers and users with reliable access to the right models. The gateway goes beyond simply managing AI traffic by intelligently routing each request to the most appropriate model based on the needs of that request. It also provides key capabilities such as identity-based AI access, smart routing, and centralized traffic and cost management.

The A10 AI Gateway is part of A10's broader AI security portfolio, which includes TrojAI and ThreatX solutions. The portfolio secures AI across its full lifecycle, from testing and deployment to runtime and network enforcement. The A10 AI Gateway is delivered as software or integrated hardware that runs entirely within the customer's own environment, ensuring full data sovereignty and control over models, data, and policy. This approach provides a more controlled, efficient, and secure approach to enterprise AI, helping organizations maximize the value of every AI interaction while maintaining visibility and control over how AI is accessed and used.
