### Executive Summary
Researchers uncovered a credential‑theft campaign that compromised two high‑profile open‑source maintainers, including Takashi Kitao of the pyxel game engine. The attacker used Kitao’s account to push a malicious GitHub Actions workflow into 27 repositories, and later infected over 340 repositories. The workflow exfiltrates credentials and other secrets.
