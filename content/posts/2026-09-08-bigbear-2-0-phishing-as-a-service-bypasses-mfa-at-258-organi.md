### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity firm CloudSEK uncovered BigBear 2.0, a phishing-as-a-service platform that bypassed MFA on Microsoft 365, compromising over 5,000 credentials across 258 organizations. The service operated 42 VPS nodes targeting Microsoft 365, and researchers gained admin access to its control panel.
