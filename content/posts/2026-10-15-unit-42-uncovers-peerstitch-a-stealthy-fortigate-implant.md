Unit 42 investigated an intrusion that exploited CVE-2024-47575 to compromise over 500 FortiGate firewalls. The attackers deployed PEERSTITCH, a memory‑resident ARM64 malware that remains stealthy in RAM. The report details the vulnerability, attack chain, and mitigation steps.
