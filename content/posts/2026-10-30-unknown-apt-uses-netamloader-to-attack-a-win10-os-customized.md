### Executive Summary
Kaspersky researchers uncovered a sophisticated targeted campaign that leveraged the NetamLoader malware framework to compromise a Windows 10‑based operating system customized for Asian government agencies. The attack employed advanced persistence, credential theft, and lateral movement techniques to exfiltrate sensitive data. The operation demonstrates the APT’s focus on high‑value government targets and its use of a modular, stealthy loader to evade detection.
