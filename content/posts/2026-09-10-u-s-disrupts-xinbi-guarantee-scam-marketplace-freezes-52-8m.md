### Executive Summary
The U.S. Department of Justice announced a coordinated operation that shut down the Xinbi Guarantee scam marketplace. Authorities seized the Telegram channels that ran the service, confiscated two cryptocurrency wallets, and deployed the Scam Center Strike Force to Madagascar to dismantle 13 scam compounds operated by Chinese organized crime. The action froze $52.8 million in crypto assets.
