### Executive Summary
Railway, a cloud hosting provider, was breached when attackers exploited infrastructure vulnerabilities to steal customer authentication secrets. They used the stolen credentials to generate about $740,000 in fraudulent AI usage, potentially exposing credentials for up to 100,000 workspaces.
