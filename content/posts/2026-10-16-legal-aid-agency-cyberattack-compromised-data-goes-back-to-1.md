### Executive Summary
In April 2025, the UK’s Legal Aid Agency suffered a cyberattack that exposed personal data dating back to 1978. The breach, first reported earlier this year, now reveals that compromised records span over four decades, affecting millions of individuals. The agency announced the extent of the data loss and is working with authorities to mitigate the impact.
