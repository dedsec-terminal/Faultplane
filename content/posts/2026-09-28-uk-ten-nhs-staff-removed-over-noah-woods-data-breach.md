### Executive Summary
Ten NHS staff were removed from duty or suspended after a data breach exposed the digital medical records of three‑year‑old Noah Woods. An urgent investigation was launched by East Suffolk and North Essex NHS Foundation Trust, with Deputy Chief Medical Officer Dr Martin Mansfield stating that any unauthorised access of the records is being investigated.
