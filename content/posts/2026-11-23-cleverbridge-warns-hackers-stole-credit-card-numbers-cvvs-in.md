### Executive Summary
E‑commerce platform Cleverbridge announced a data breach in which attackers accessed customer payment card data, including full credit card numbers and CVV codes. The breach was discovered after unusual activity was detected, and the company is notifying affected users and advising them to monitor accounts and change passwords. No evidence of account compromise was reported, but the incident highlights the risk of payment data theft.
