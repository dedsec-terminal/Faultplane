### Executive Summary
A teenager from Amman, Jordan, suspected of leading the ShinyHunters data‑theft and extortion gang, was detained and is cooperating with the FBI. The suspect, known as "Rey", was arrested while the group was extorting a recently divested Boeing business unit. The extortion involved a unit that supplies aircraft to Royal Jordanian Airlines, where Rey’s father works.
