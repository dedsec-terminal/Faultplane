### Executive Summary
Cold‑storage facilities used by France’s national blood service were targeted in a cyberattack that reportedly programmed units containing blood samples to reach 110 °C (230 °F). The attack occurred overnight from Sunday to Monday, as reported by OMM, Le Canard enchaîné and Anadolu Agency.
