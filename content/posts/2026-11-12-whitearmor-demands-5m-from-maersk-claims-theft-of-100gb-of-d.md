### Executive Summary
WhiteArmor, a cyber threat actor, has demanded $5 million from shipping giant Maersk, alleging it stole 100 GB of data from a legacy system inherited from a prior acquisition. Maersk confirmed an IT security incident involving unauthorized access and data extraction from the system. The incident highlights risks associated with legacy infrastructure and the growing trend of ransomware‑style extortion.
