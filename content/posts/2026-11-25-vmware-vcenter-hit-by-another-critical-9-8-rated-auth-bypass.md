### Executive Summary
Broadcom has released patches for 23 vulnerabilities in VMware products, including a critical 9.8‑rated authentication bypass flaw (CVE‑2026‑123903) in VMware Directory Service (vmdir). The flaw, exploited as a zero‑day, allows attackers to bypass authentication in vCenter, ESX, Workstation, Fusion, and Avi Load Balancer. VMware and Broadcom urge users to apply the updates immediately.
