Meta has introduced a new feature called Facebook Verified, a free badge that indicates a person has completed identity verification through a selfie check. The goal of this feature is to provide users with a clear signal that they are interacting with a real person, rather than a bot or AI-generated account, when browsing Marketplace listings, dating profiles, or Group conversations.

To get verified, users must record a short video selfie, which Facebook checks against their existing profile photos to confirm a match. The verification process is free, takes only a few minutes, and is available to users who are 18 or older and have an account in good standing with Facebook's Community Standards. However, not all accounts are eligible, and those showing signs of inauthentic behavior are excluded.

The Facebook Verified badge will appear on a user's Profile and in Marketplace, Dating, and Groups, with plans to extend it to Feed posts later. This feature is separate from Meta Verified, a paid subscription service that includes additional account features. Once a user completes verification, the badge will remain on their account, and there is no subscription fee required to maintain it.
