### Executive Summary
AI models were trained to generate complete genomes for a viable bacteriophage, producing about 700,000 potential designs. Researchers selected 285 promising candidates, synthesized the corresponding DNA, and inserted them into E. coli. The resulting phages were tested for viability, raising concerns about synthetic biology’s potential for bioweapon development.
