### Executive Summary
Microsoft’s technical analysis revealed that attackers used the NeedyMantis malware family to keep long‑term access in networks they had already breached. The tool has appeared in a handful of targeted attacks against telecom firms, universities, medical nonprofits, intergovernmental bodies, and government contractors, with activity traced back at least to early 2023.
