### Executive Summary
DataBreaches reports that an international operation coordinated by Eurojust and Europol, involving nine countries, has arrested teenagers suspected of leading the KillSec ransomware group. The group has been active for nearly two years, targeting victims with ransomware attacks. The arrests mark a significant blow to the KillSec campaign.
