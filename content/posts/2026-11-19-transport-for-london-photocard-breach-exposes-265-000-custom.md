### Executive Summary
Transport for London (TfL) confirmed that personal details of roughly 265,000 Oyster photocard holders were accessed in a cyber breach. Compromised data included names, email addresses, home addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and security questions and answers. The breach was detected after suspicious activity on the photocard website.
