### Executive Summary
On Oct 20-21, hackers targeted Alpine Linux's development GitLab instance, attempting to exploit a critical GitLab vulnerability. The project responded by rebuilding the GitLab server and resetting all passwords to mitigate potential compromise. No evidence of data exfiltration was reported.
