### Executive Summary
A proxyjacking campaign has compromised D-Link and Ubiquiti routers in 34 countries, turning infected devices into commercial residential proxies that sell victims' internet bandwidth. Attackers use compromised devices to route traffic, generating revenue for the threat actors. The campaign highlights the vulnerability of consumer‑grade routers to hijacking and the growing threat of bandwidth theft.
