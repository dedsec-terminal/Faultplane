### Executive Summary
Hackers compromised AOMEI's update servers and used the software's built‑in promotional feature to deliver malware. They briefly altered configuration files, then restored them to cover their tracks. The attack targeted the backup software's update mechanism, allowing the malicious payload to reach users during normal update checks.
