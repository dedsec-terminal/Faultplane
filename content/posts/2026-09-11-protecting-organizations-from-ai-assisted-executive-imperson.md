### Executive Summary
Microsoft Security Blog reports on a new business email compromise (BEC) campaign that leveraged generative AI to craft convincing executive impersonation emails and fake invoices. The attackers targeted finance teams, requesting ACH payments to fraudulent accounts. The post outlines detection tactics, mitigation steps, and the importance of verifying invoice authenticity and employee training to defend against AI‑enhanced fraud.
