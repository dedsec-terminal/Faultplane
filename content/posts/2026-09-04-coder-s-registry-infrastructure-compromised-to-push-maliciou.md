### Executive Summary
Attackers hijacked Coder’s Cloudflare infrastructure, inserting unauthorized registry servers that served malicious Terraform modules. The modules contained credential‑stealing code, enabling attackers to harvest secrets from users’ environments. The compromise highlights the risk of third‑party registry services and the need for stricter access controls.
