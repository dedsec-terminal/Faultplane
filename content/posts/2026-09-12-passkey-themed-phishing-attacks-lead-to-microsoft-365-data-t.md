### Executive Summary
Threat actors linked to ShinyHunters, Helix, and other extortion gangs are exploiting passkey and single sign‑on themes in social engineering campaigns to compromise corporate Microsoft accounts. By tricking users into revealing credentials, attackers gain access to Microsoft 365 services and exfiltrate sensitive data. Microsoft has warned organizations to verify authentication requests and strengthen security controls.
