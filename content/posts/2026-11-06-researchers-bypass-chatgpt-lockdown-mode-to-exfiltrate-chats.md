### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers revealed a vulnerability that bypasses OpenAI’s ChatGPT Lockdown Mode, allowing attackers to silently exfiltrate chat content without user interaction. The flaw, discovered in June 2026, could enable data theft and phishing via prompt injection. OpenAI has patched the issue, but the incident highlights ongoing risks of AI‑driven data leakage.
