### Executive Summary
Microsoft Security reports that the AI‑powered penetration testing framework ARTEX is being abused by threat actors such as Storm‑0940, Storm‑3358, Onyx Sleet, Pearl Sleet, Crystal Typhoon, and Salt Typhoon. The attackers use ARTEX to accelerate network reconnaissance, credential theft, and web application exploitation, streamlining their attack workflows.
