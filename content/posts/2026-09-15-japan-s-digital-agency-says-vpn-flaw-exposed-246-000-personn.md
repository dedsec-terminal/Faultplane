### Executive Summary
Japan's Digital Agency revealed that a flaw in its VPN system exposed roughly 246,000 rows of personnel data, including names, positions, and contact details of government employees. The breach was discovered after routine security checks flagged unusual traffic. Agency said it has isolated the affected systems and is notifying affected staff, but the full extent of data accessed remains unclear. The incident underscores the risk of misconfigured VPNs in protecting sensitive public sector information.
