Here are three concise paragraphs summarizing the article:

Cisco has released updates to address multiple critical security vulnerabilities in its Catalyst SD-WAN and IOS XE Software. The vulnerabilities were discovered during internal security testing and are not known to be actively exploited. A total of 12 flaws were patched, including three with a CVSS score of 9.8 or higher, which could allow attackers to gain unauthorized access or execute arbitrary commands.

The vulnerabilities affect Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Software and IOS XE Software, with the former impacted by flaws such as improper input validation, access control, and link resolution. The issues have been addressed in various versions of the software, with customers urged to apply the necessary updates for optimal protection. The IOS XE Software vulnerabilities relate to improper access control, command injection, and input validation, and have been fixed in several versions of the software.

In addition to the SD-WAN and IOS XE vulnerabilities, Cisco also patched a high-severity flaw in the web-based management interface of its Integrated Management Controller (IMC). The vulnerability, which has a CVSS score of 8.8, could allow an authenticated attacker to execute arbitrary commands and elevate privileges to root. A proof-of-concept exploit is available, and security researchers have warned that a compromise of the IMC could have significant consequences, allowing attackers to nest themselves deeply and persistently in the system.
