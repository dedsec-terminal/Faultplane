### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs reports that fake Calendly pages masquerading as Meta Careers invitations lure users into entering Facebook and Google credentials, even when two-factor authentication is enabled. The malicious pages capture login details, enabling attackers to compromise accounts. The threat demonstrates how social engineering can bypass 2FA by tricking users into authenticating on a fraudulent site.
