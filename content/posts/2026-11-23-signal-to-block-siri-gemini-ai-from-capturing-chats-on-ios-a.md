### Executive Summary
Signal has introduced new privacy safeguards that block screenshot functionality on iOS 27 and prevent AI assistants such as Siri and Gemini AI from accessing or recording conversations on supported Android devices. The update aims to protect user privacy by ensuring that chat content cannot be captured by third‑party assistants or through screenshots, enhancing security for encrypted messaging on both platforms.
