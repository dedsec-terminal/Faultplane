### Executive Summary
Cisco Talos reports that attackers are exploiting over 25 trusted platforms—including collaboration, file‑sharing, payment, and other services—to deliver malware and other malicious payloads. By leveraging the inherent trust in these legitimate infrastructures, adversaries bypass security controls and reach targets more effectively.
