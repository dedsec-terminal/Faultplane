### Executive Summary
OpenAI announced it will double the usage limits for its ChatGPT Pro plans ($100, $200, $500) and Business Premium, following user backlash over a proposed 50% cut. The company also pledged to keep Dots usage independent. The move aims to address concerns about reduced access and maintain service quality.
