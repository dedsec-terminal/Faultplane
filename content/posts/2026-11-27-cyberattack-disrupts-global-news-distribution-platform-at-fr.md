### Executive Summary

French international news agency Agence France-Presse (AFP) was hit by a cyberattack Thursday, forcing it to take offline one of its main platforms for distributing news content to media organizations worldwide. The attack targeted AFP Forum, an online platform that news outlets use to browse and download the agency’s stories, photographs and videos. AFP confirmed the incident Thursday in an internal email to employees, which was quoted in the agency’s own reporting. The agency is investigating the attack with assistance from external cybersecurity experts. “AFP was hit by a cyber attack overnight.
