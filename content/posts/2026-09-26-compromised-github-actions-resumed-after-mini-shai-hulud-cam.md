### Executive Summary
Two GitHub Actions repositories—actions-cool/issues-helper and actions-cool/maintain-one-comment—were disabled again after being re‑enabled last week. The repos were compromised during the May 2026 Mini Shai-Hulud malware campaign. The reactivation has prompted a second disabling, indicating ongoing threat activity. The incident highlights the persistence of malicious actors exploiting CI/CD pipelines.
