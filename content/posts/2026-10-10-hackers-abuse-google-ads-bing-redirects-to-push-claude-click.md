### Executive Summary
Cybercriminals exploit legitimate Bing search-result redirects as click URLs in Google search ads, steering users to counterfeit Claude AI installers that host ClickFix malware. The scheme uses Google Ads to drive traffic, then redirects via Bing to malicious sites, delivering a trojan that installs a backdoor and steals credentials. The attack demonstrates how search ad platforms can be weaponized for malware distribution.
