### Executive Summary
Ascent Skilled Nursing Facilities (Asheville) notified residents that a threat actor accessed their systems and stole data. The breach involved Bear Mountain Health & Rehabilitation, Elevate Health & Rehabilitation, and Swannanoa Valley Health & Rehabilitation. The company claimed the stolen data was deleted and provided residents with assurances and guidance.
