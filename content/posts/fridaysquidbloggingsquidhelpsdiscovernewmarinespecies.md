A recent scientific expedition utilized a cutting-edge machine called the Squid, a spinning wheel confocal microscope, to discover new marine species. The Squid uses lasers to scan microscopic details of organisms, allowing for real-time analysis of cellular interactions and skeleton building. This technology enabled the team to explore the marine world in unprecedented detail.

The expedition was highly successful, resulting in the discovery of 31 new marine species over a period of just two weeks. The use of the Squid machine was a key factor in this success, as it allowed the team to analyze samples immediately, rather than waiting weeks for laboratory results. The Squid's capabilities have opened up new possibilities for marine research and exploration.
