### Executive Summary

The DFIR report claims that a nearly two‑month intrusion was enabled by the Lunar Spider malware, detailing the full attack lifecycle from initial access to exfiltration. It outlines tactics such as execution, persistence, privilege escalation, defense evasion, credential access, discovery, lateral movement, command‑and‑control, and data exfiltration, and provides a timeline, diamond model analysis, and MITRE ATT&CK mapping. The report offers indicators and detection guidance to help security teams identify and mitigate similar attacks. No specific victims or impact details are disclosed in the source.
