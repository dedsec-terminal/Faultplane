### Executive Summary
The Silent Ransom Group (SRG) targeted the merged law firm Hogan Lovells Cadwalader, a New York City-based firm formed from the 2022 merger of Cadwalader, Wickersham & Taft and Hogan Lovells. SRG launched a ransomware attack, demanding payment. After the firm refused to pay, SRG re‑attacked, further compromising data. The incident highlights the growing threat to legal firms and the persistence of ransomware operators.
