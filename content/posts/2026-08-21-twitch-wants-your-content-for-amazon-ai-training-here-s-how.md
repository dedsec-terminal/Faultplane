### Executive Summary
Twitch has introduced an opt‑out option that lets users prevent their uploaded videos from being used to train Amazon’s AI models. The feature follows a 2022 disclosure that Twitch had already begun using user content for Amazon’s training. The new setting is available in the Twitch settings menu and allows creators to opt out of the data collection. Twitch says the opt‑out does not affect the normal operation of the platform.
