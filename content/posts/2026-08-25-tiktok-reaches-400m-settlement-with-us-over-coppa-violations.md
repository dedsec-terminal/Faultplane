### Executive Summary
The U.S. Department of Justice announced a $400 million settlement with TikTok, ByteDance, and affiliated companies after allegations that they violated the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The settlement requires TikTok to implement stricter privacy safeguards for children, pay the fine, and submit to DOJ oversight for a period of time.
