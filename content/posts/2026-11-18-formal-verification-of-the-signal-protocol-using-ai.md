### Executive Summary
Google DeepMind researchers used agentic AI to formally verify key cryptographic guarantees of the Signal protocol in a four‑week proof‑of‑concept. The work independently validates the cryptography behind Signal, Google Messages, and WhatsApp, and showcases DeepMind’s AlphaProof Code framework for formal verification.
