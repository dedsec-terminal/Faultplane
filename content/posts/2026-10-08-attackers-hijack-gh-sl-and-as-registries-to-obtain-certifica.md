### Executive Summary
In early October, attackers compromised the registries for Ghana (.gh), Sierra Leone (.sl), and American Samoa (.as), enabling them to issue unauthorized HTTPS certificates for multiple Google domains. Google confirmed the breach on Oct 6, noting that its own systems were not affected. The compromised certificates could allow attackers to impersonate Google sites over encrypted connections, posing a significant security risk for any domain ending in the affected ccTLDs.
