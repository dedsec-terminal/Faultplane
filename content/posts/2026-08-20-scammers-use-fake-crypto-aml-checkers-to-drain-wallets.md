### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs discovered fake anti-money laundering (AML) checker sites that masquerade as legitimate crypto wallet verification services. These sites trick users into granting access, enabling scammers to drain their wallets. The operation exploits trust in AML tools to facilitate fraud.
