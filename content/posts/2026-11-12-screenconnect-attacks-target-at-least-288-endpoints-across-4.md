### Executive Summary
ConnectWise has disabled five third‑party extensions in partner environments after reports of unauthorized access via rogue ScreenConnect clients. The advisory states no evidence of infrastructure compromise or software vulnerability. The incident affected at least 288 endpoints in 48 organizations.
