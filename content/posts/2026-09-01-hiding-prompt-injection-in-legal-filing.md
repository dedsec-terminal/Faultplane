### Executive Summary
A recent incident revealed that an attacker embedded malicious AI prompt injection instructions within a legal filing document. By disguising the instructions as legitimate text, the attacker aimed to manipulate AI systems that process legal documents, potentially altering outcomes or extracting sensitive data. The case highlights the growing risk of prompt injection attacks in legal and regulatory contexts.
