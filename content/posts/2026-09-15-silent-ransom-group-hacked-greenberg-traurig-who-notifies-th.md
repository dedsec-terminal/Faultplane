### Executive Summary
Silent Ransom Group (SRG) added Greenberg Traurig to its list of targeted law firms and published a leak of the firm's data. The breach was reported by DataBreaches.net, which also covered a separate incident involving Troutman Pepper Locke. SRG’s leak site listed 64 law firms, including Greenberg Traurig, with 126,000 affected individuals. The article details the notification process and the scope of the compromise.
