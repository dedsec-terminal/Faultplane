### Executive Summary
Japan's RIKEN Center for Computational Science has taken its Fugaku supercomputer offline after detecting a security breach. The incident, reported by Bleeping Computer, highlights vulnerabilities in high-performance computing systems. The supercomputer, one of the world's fastest, was temporarily shut down to mitigate potential damage and investigate the attack.
