### Executive Summary
Microsoft Security observed phishing campaigns that leveraged the MSP360 Remote Management (RMM) tool to deploy the remote‑access software ScreenConnect. The attackers used the RMM channel to establish redundant remote‑access pathways, enabling persistent footholds for subsequent malicious activity.
