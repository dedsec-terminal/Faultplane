### Executive Summary
The Russian Ministry of Finance is exploring mandatory cyber‑risk insurance for businesses tied to critical infrastructure, applying a principle used for hazardous facilities. Deputy Finance Minister Ivan Chebeskov announced the proposal at a Moscow event, suggesting cyberattacks could be insured like accidents.
