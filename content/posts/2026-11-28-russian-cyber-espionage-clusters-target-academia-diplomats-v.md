### Executive Summary

Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) has identified three suspected Russian threat clusters—UNC6293, UNC7005, and UNC5976—that target individuals in academia, aerospace, defense, government, and think‑tank sectors across Europe and the United States. The groups employ sophisticated social engineering, including app‑password phishing that impersonates U.S. State Department officials and OAuth phishing that lures victims into submitting verification codes on legitimate‑looking sites. GTIG reports that these campaigns have compromised personal accounts by exploiting legitimate authentication workflows, with UNC6293’s operations noted as a sub‑cluster of ICE RELIC (APT29) and UNC7005’s activities linked to hospitality captive‑portal redirects. Defensive guidance from GTIG urges users to verify the authenticity of any request for app passwords or verification codes, remain vigilant for phishing documents that mimic official communications, and avoid sharing credentials via email or untrusted forms.
