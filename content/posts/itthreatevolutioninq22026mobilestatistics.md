Here are three concise paragraphs summarizing the IT threat evolution in Q2 2026, focusing on mobile statistics:

In Q2 2026, Kaspersky Security Network reported a decline in attacks on mobile devices, with 1,996,823 attacks blocked, down from 2,676,328 in the previous quarter. The most prevalent mobile malware threat was the Trojan-Banker category, accounting for 30.77% of total detected applications. More than 304,000 malicious installation packages were discovered, including 93,574 related to mobile banking Trojans and 570 related to mobile ransomware Trojans.

The report highlights a shift in tactics by threat actors, with a decrease in pre-installed Trojans and an increase in malicious loaders hosted on Google Play. One notable case involved a PDF reader app trojanized to drop the Anatsa banking malware, while another case involved a loader detected in the Cleanova app that sent requests to a command-and-control server to gather telemetry and return a malicious payload. The number of Android malware samples remained steady, with 304,128 samples detected in Q2.

The top 20 most frequently detected types of mobile malware were dominated by banking Trojans, with the Mamont banking Trojan climbing the leaderboards. The total volume of Trojan-Banker applications dropped sharply, but the distribution shifted heavily toward Creduz Trojans. However, the proportion of targeted users continued to be dominated by diverse Mamont variants. The report notes that threat actors are actively iterating on their malware, generating new builds and testing new features, which may lead to future increases in attacks.
