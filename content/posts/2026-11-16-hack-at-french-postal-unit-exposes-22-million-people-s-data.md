### Executive Summary
A cyberattack on Maileva, the digital subsidiary of France’s La Poste Group, occurred in mid‑September and exposed the first names, last names and postal addresses of roughly 22 million individuals. The breach was confirmed by La Poste and reported by AFP and Türkiye Today.
