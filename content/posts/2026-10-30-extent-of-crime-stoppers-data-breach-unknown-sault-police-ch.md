### Executive Summary
Sault Ste. Marie Police chief reports uncertainty over the number of anonymous tips potentially exposed in a large data breach involving the local Crime Stoppers chapter. In response, both the Sault Ste. Marie Police Service and the Anishinabek Police Service have suspended their partnership with the chapter.
