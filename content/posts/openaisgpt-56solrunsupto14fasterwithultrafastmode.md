OpenAI has introduced Ultrafast mode for its GPT-5.6 Sol model, which runs up to 14 times faster than the standard processing mode. This mode is currently available in limited preview to select customers through the OpenAI API and is powered by Cerebras as part of their partnership on ultra-low-latency inference. With Ultrafast mode, the model can generate up to 750 output tokens per second.

The Ultrafast mode is being tested by customers in various applications, including coding, commerce, financial research, and support. OpenAI aims to assess where the significant increase in speed delivers the most value and how products change when models can respond quickly to user interactions. Customers have reported positive experiences, with one user noting that the increased speed enables different ways of using the models and makes it practical for developers to work more productively alongside them.

OpenAI is also internally utilizing Ultrafast mode for incident response and research workflows. The faster inference time allows for quicker analysis and decision-making, enabling engineers to respond more rapidly to incidents and complete multiple research iterations during the workday. The company believes that the increased speed will lead to more efficient and productive workflows, and is exploring ways to apply Ultrafast mode to various applications and use cases.
