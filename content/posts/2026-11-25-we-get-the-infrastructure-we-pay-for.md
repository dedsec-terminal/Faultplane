### Executive Summary
The essay discusses how the United States' critical infrastructure—ranging from aviation and banking to federal agencies—is increasingly fragile and poorly maintained. It attributes recent high-profile outages to a combination of aging systems, patch lag, and reliance on volunteer maintenance, rather than coordinated cyberattacks. The author argues that the lack of investment and centralization of key services creates systemic risk, and that the failures are likely to worsen if current practices persist.
