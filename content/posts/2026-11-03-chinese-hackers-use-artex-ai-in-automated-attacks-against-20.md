### Executive Summary
Chinese threat actors deployed the open‑source, multi‑agent AI penetration‑testing framework ARTEX to launch automated attacks on more than 200 organizations in 14 countries. The framework enabled rapid reconnaissance, exploitation of known vulnerabilities, lateral movement, and data exfiltration, allowing attackers to scale operations across diverse environments. The activity was reported by Bleeping Computer and highlights the growing use of AI tools in cyber‑espionage and ransomware campaigns.
