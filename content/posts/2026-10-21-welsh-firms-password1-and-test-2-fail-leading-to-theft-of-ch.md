### Executive Summary
Two Welsh organisations, Ogi Networks Limited and Data Cymru, were censured by the Information Commissioner’s Office after a ransomware attack compromised personal data of 3,300 individuals, most of whom were children. The breach exposed sensitive information and highlighted inadequate security measures.
