### Executive Summary
A stack-based buffer overflow in the spamd statushdlr component of WatchGuard FireWare OS allows authenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary code. The vulnerability, identified as CVE-2026-18145 and assigned CVSS 7.2, requires authentication and was disclosed by Zero Day Initiative (ZDI-26-750).
