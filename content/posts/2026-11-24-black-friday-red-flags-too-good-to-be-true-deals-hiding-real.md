### Executive Summary
Cybercriminals are ramping up phishing, scams, and malware attacks around Black Friday, targeting online shoppers and gamers. Schemes include fake GTA VI promotions, wellness product fraud, and spam emails promising deep discounts. Attackers exploit the holiday shopping frenzy to distribute malicious payloads and harvest credentials.
