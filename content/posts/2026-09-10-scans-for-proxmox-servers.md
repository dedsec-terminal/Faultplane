### Executive Summary
Proxmox issued an advisory about a vulnerability in its older Proxmox VE version 7, which has not been supported for several years. The flaw could be exploited by attackers scanning for vulnerable Proxmox servers, potentially allowing unauthorized access or privilege escalation. The advisory urges administrators to upgrade to supported versions or apply mitigations to protect their environments.
