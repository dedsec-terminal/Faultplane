### Executive Summary

Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 reports that since August 2026, Com‑affiliated threat actors have launched multiple waves of vishing attacks aimed at hijacking cloud accounts. The attacks targeted financial services, healthcare, legal services, and construction firms across the U.S., U.K., and Australia, resulting in data theft and extortion attempts. Unit 42 has tracked and responded to these incidents, highlighting the growing use of social‑engineering tactics to compromise cloud environments.
