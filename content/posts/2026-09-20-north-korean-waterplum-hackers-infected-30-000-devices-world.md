### Executive Summary
The WaterPlum hacking group, linked to North Korea, compromised at least 30,000 devices globally between December 2025 and July 2026. They exfiltrated data and transferred over $10.7 million in stolen cryptocurrency to North Korean accounts, according to a joint law‑enforcement advisory. The incident highlights the group's expanding reach and financial motives.
