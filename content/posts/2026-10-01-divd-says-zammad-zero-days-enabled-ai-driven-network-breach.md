### Executive Summary
The Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure (DIVD) reported that attackers breached its network by exploiting a chain of two zero‑day vulnerabilities in the open‑source Zammad ticketing system. The exploitation was reportedly AI‑driven, allowing the attackers to gain unauthorized access and potentially exfiltrate data. The incident highlights the risk of unpatched zero‑days in widely used open‑source software.
