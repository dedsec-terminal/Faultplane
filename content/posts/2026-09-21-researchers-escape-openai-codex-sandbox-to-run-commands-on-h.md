### Executive Summary
Researchers discovered two methods to escape the OpenAI Codex sandbox, enabling execution of arbitrary commands on a developer’s machine even from the platform’s most restricted mode. The exploits allowed direct host access, posing a significant security risk. OpenAI has since patched both vulnerabilities, restoring sandbox isolation.
