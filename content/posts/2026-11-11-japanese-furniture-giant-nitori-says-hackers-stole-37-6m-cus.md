### Executive Summary
Japanese furniture retailer Nitori revealed that an attacker accessed a system on its network and may have stolen 37.62 million customer records, including personal and payment information. The breach was discovered during routine security checks, and Nitori is investigating the extent of the compromise and notifying affected customers.
