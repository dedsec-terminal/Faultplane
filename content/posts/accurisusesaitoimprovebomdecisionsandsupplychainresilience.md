Accuris has introduced new AI capabilities for its BOM Intelligence platform, aiming to enhance decision-making and supply chain resilience. The platform utilizes verified data covering 1.3 billion electronic components with 98%+ accuracy, built on 35+ years of direct manufacturer relationships. This foundation enables AI to compress weeks of impact analysis into hours, providing results that can withstand audit and scrutiny.

The new AI capabilities are designed to support supply chain and engineering teams in making better BOM decisions, monitoring risk, and forecasting compliance and obsolescence. The platform offers three key areas of support: AI-powered BOM optimization, continuous BOM health monitoring, and predictive compliance and risk intelligence. These capabilities help teams choose the right parts, identify potential risks, and anticipate regulatory changes, ultimately reducing costs and improving efficiency.

The introduction of these AI capabilities is expected to bring significant value to customers, particularly in industries such as aerospace and defense, automotive, medical devices, and manufacturing. By automating analysis and surfacing risk, Accuris' platform enables teams to make informed decisions, reduce rework costs, and improve sourcing decisions. The company's approach prioritizes keeping teams in control, with AI accelerating analysis and providing recommendations, while human teams make the final decisions. This approach is expected to establish a new standard for AI in engineering and supply chain intelligence.
