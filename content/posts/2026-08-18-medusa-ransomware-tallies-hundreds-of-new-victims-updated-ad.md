### Executive Summary
The ransomware-as-a-service group Medusa has expanded its operations, adding hundreds of new victims in just over a year. A U.S. government advisory notes the gang now uses access brokers, paying $100–$1 million for compromised credentials, and has adopted new tactics to gain entry. The update highlights the growing threat posed by Medusa and its evolving attack methods.
