### Executive Summary
The Data Protection Commission fined the Health Service Executive (HSE) €645,000 for mishandling historical paper records at St Loman's Hospital in Mullingar and St Conal's Hospital in Letterkenny. The fine followed an inquiry into the improper management of patient data at the two facilities.
