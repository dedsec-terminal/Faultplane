### Executive Summary
A cybercriminal network compromised more than 5,400 small‑business websites to host and deliver ClickFix malware. The payloads are stored in smart contracts on the BNB Smart Chain, allowing attackers to serve malicious code via compromised sites. The operation demonstrates a novel use of blockchain for malware distribution and highlights the scale of web‑site hijacking.
