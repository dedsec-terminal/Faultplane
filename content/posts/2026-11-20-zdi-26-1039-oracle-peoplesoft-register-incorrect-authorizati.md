### Executive Summary
A vulnerability in Oracle PeopleSoft's register component allows authenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary code. The flaw, identified by Zero Day Initiative as ZDI-26-1039, carries a CVSS score of 9.9 and is assigned CVE-2026-35273. Exploitation requires valid credentials but can lead to full system compromise.
