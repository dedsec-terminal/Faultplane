### Executive Summary
Comcast has introduced motion‑detection functionality in its Xfinity wireless routers. The feature alerts users via push notifications when motion is detected near connected devices such as TVs or printers, with settings for home, asleep, or away modes. The Xfinity app displays live activity and a recent‑activity feed. Comcast notes that detection accuracy varies with home size, layout, building materials, and device placement, and does not guarantee performance.
