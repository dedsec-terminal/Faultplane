### Executive Summary
The FTC rescinded its 2021 Policy Statement on Breaches by Health Apps and Other Connected Devices, which had applied the Health Breach Notification Rule to health apps and connected devices that collect consumer health information. The policy was deemed obsolete and replaced with updated guidance in 2024.
