### Executive Summary
Three user accounts at Danish IT firm Pays, including an administrator, used the weak password '123456'. Hackers exploited these credentials to gain unauthorized access to the Denmark Central Person Register (CPR), exposing personal data of millions. The breach highlights the risks of default or simple passwords in critical national databases.
