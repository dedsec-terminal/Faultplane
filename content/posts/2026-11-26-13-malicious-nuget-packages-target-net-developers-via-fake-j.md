### Executive Summary
Researchers uncovered 13 malicious NuGet packages masquerading as WinUI 3 and WPF button controls. The packages lure Windows .NET developers by presenting a fake take‑home job assessment, then deliver malware when built or run. The discovery highlights the risk of third‑party package supply chains.
