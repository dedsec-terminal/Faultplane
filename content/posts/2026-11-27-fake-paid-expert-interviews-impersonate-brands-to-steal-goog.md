### Executive Summary
A phishing operation dubbed "GoodGuys" is targeting corporate users by sending fake invitations to paid expert interviews and webinars. The emails impersonate well‑known brands such as Google, Nvidia, Accenture, and SpaceX, luring recipients into providing their Google and Okta credentials. The campaign exploits the trust associated with these brands to harvest login information for corporate accounts.
