### Executive Summary
Swiss cybersecurity agency confirmed that attackers using AI agents breached computer systems at five Swiss organizations, including three critical infrastructure operators. Two of those breaches succeeded, but no major disruptions were reported. Victims remain unnamed and the perpetrators are unknown.
