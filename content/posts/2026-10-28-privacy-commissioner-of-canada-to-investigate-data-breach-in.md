### Executive Summary
The Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Philippe Dufresne, has opened an investigation into a cybersecurity incident involving the tip‑management platform used by Canadian Crime Stoppers chapters. The probe targets Navigate360, the commercial organization that provides and manages the platform, after a potential data breach of tips shared with Crime Stoppers programs across Canada.
