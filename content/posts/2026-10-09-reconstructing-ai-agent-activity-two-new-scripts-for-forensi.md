### Executive Summary
The article reports a major update to the FOR577 course, adding new material on day 5 that focuses on investigating AI usage in incident response. It covers eight popular AI coding assistants—Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Copilot, Warp, Windsurf, and Qwen Code—and details the author’s experience with Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and Hermes. The author explains how to locate chat history in OpenCode and identify evidence related to Hermes, providing practical guidance for forensic analysts.
