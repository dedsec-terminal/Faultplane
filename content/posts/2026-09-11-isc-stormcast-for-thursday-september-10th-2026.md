### Executive Summary
The SANS Internet Storm Center released its weekly stormcast for Thursday, September 10th, 2026, summarizing the latest global threat landscape. The report highlighted increased phishing campaigns targeting financial institutions, a surge in DDoS traffic from compromised IoT devices, and new variants of the Emotet malware spreading via malicious email attachments. Analysts noted a spike in exploitation attempts against unpatched Windows SMB services and a rise in ransomware‑as‑a‑service activity. The ISC urged organizations to review patch management, strengthen email filtering, and monitor outbound traffic for anomalous patterns.
