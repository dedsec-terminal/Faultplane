### Executive Summary

The article presents the 18th annual community‑powered list of the most innovative web‑application security research published in 2024. After a three‑step process—community nominations, voting, and expert panel review—the final top ten include techniques such as Cookie Tossing, Web Cache Deception, CVE‑2024‑4367 (arbitrary JavaScript execution in PDF.js), and DoubleClickjacking. The piece highlights how these attacks exploit weaknesses in cookie handling, caching, and UI redressing, underscoring the need for stronger cookie policies, cache rule enforcement, and input sanitisation. While the article does not prescribe specific mitigations, it encourages defenders to review and harden the identified attack surfaces.
