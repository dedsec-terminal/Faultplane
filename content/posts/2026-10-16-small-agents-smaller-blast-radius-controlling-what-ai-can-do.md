### Executive Summary
The article argues that the cybersecurity community is tackling AI security incorrectly, focusing on broad defenses rather than limiting AI capabilities. It proposes a strategy of deploying small, tightly scoped AI agents with a reduced blast radius to mitigate risks. By constraining what AI can do, organizations can better manage potential misuse and reduce the attack surface. The piece calls for a shift toward granular control and responsible AI design in security operations.
