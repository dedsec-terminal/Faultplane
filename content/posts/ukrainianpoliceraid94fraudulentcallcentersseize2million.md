Ukrainian police have conducted a nationwide operation, raiding 94 fraudulent call centers and seizing over $2 million in assets. The call centers were involved in various schemes, including impersonating bank employees, offering fraudulent investment services, and attempting to gain remote access to victims' devices. The operation, which involved over 400 searches, resulted in the seizure of thousands of computers, phones, and SIM cards, as well as 22 cars and jewelry.

The call centers used various tactics to target their victims, including pretending to work for banks, offering investment opportunities, and selling dietary supplements with false therapeutic claims. Some operators also targeted people outside Ukraine, including EU citizens, and used cryptocurrency exchanges to launder stolen funds. The police have identified 67 financial instruments believed to have been used in the schemes, including bank accounts, cryptocurrency wallets, and suspected money mules.

The operation has led to 26 people being notified that they are suspected of criminal offenses, and the investigations are proceeding under Ukraine's Criminal Code covering fraud and money laundering. The offenses can carry prison sentences of up to 12 years and confiscation of property. The Ukrainian police are working with other authorities, including the Security Service of Ukraine and the Office of the Prosecutor General, to examine the seized material, trace financial transactions, and identify further participants in the schemes.
