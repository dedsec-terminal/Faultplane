### Executive Summary
New York Attorney General Letitia James and Connecticut Attorney General William Tong obtained a $2.7 million settlement from Boston Children’s Health Physicians (BCHP) and its IT vendor XTIUM following a 2024 data breach. The agreement includes a corrective action plan to strengthen data security and holds both the provider and the IT company liable for the breach.
