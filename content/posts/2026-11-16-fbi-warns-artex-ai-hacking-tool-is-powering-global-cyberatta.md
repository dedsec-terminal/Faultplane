### Executive Summary
The FBI has issued a flash advisory warning that the open‑source AI‑powered penetration testing framework ARTEX is being weaponized by threat actors to automate cyberattacks worldwide. Recent incidents include a ConnectWise supply‑chain breach that exposed Microsoft 365 customer environments. The agency notes that ARTEX has been in use by multiple actors since at least 2023.
