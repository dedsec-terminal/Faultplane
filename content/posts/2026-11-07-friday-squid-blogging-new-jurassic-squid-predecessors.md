### Executive Summary
Scientists have uncovered the largest known belemnite fossil, a barrel‑shaped squid relative from the Jurassic period that lived in what is now Wyoming. The 160‑million‑year‑old specimen, dubbed the "fattest" belemnite, shows unique features suggesting it may have preyed on smaller cephalopods and survived a lineage thought extinct. The find expands knowledge of Jurassic marine ecosystems and cephalopod evolution.
