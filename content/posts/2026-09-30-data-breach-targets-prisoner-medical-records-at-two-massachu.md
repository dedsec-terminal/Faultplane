### Executive Summary
A cybersecurity incident compromised the electronic health record system used by Suffolk County’s two jails. Computer Systems Integrated Inc., the provider of the EHRs-C platform that stores prisoner health data, confirmed the breach and is investigating. The incident could expose medical records of inmates in the jails.
