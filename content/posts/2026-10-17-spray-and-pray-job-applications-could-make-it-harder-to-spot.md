### Executive Summary
The article warns that sending a large volume of unsolicited job applications—"spray and pray"—can obscure genuine recruiter responses, making it easier for scammers to masquerade as legitimate offers. It explains how the sheer number of messages can overwhelm recipients, causing them to overlook warning signs and inadvertently engage with phishing or fraudulent job offers.
