### Executive Summary
Railway, a cloud platform for developers, issued an urgent advisory after detecting suspicious activity on systems that store customer environment variables. The company urged users to rotate secrets and review access controls to mitigate potential data exposure. Railway confirmed no confirmed breach but recommended immediate action to protect credentials.
