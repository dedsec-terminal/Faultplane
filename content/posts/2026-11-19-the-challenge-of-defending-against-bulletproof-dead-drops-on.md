### Executive Summary
Malware command‑and‑control (C2) infrastructure is moving from fragile, centralized servers and hardcoded domains to resilient, decentralized blockchain networks. These 'bulletproof dead drops' use public blockchains to store and retrieve C2 instructions, eliminating single points of failure and making detection and takedown difficult.
