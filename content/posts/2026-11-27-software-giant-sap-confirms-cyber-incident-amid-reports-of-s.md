### Executive Summary

German software giant SAP confirmed a cybersecurity incident following reports that hackers infiltrated its software development infrastructure, potentially gaining access to the company’s source code. In a statement to Recorded Future News on Friday, SAP said it had identified suspicious activity and immediately taken steps to protect its customers and secure its services.
