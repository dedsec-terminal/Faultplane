### Executive Summary
The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights (OCR) announced a settlement with Ambry Genetics Corp. over alleged HIPAA Security Rule violations. The agreement addresses potential breaches of patient data security, imposes compliance requirements, and includes penalties. Ambry, based in Aliso Viejo, California, is a covered entity under HIPAA.
