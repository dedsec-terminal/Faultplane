### Executive Summary
Citizen Lab traced zero‑click infections of Candiru spyware on Al Jazeera devices that were triggered by ordinary online ads. The malware, sold by Israeli firm Candiru to governments, executed automatically when an ad loaded—sometimes off‑screen—without any user interaction. The attack demonstrates how targeted ads can deliver spyware without clicks.
