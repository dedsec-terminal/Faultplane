### Executive Summary
WordPress removed six WP Connect plugins that were backdoored on Nov 22 2026, enabling arbitrary code execution on sites that installed them. The compromise stemmed from a suspected breach of the developer’s account. The WordPress plugins team detected and blocked the threat before the malicious releases went live.
