### Executive Summary
A cyberattack on June 29 forced the Salida School District in Colorado to shut down its network, damaging locally stored files and disrupting administrative operations. The attack began around 6:30 a.m. and was detected two hours later. The district took all systems offline and engaged specialists to restore services.
