### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs warns consumers about deceptive online advent calendar stores. The report identifies common red flags such as copied storefront designs, false urgency tactics, and contradictory product claims. These tactics aim to trick shoppers into purchasing fake or non‑existent products. The article advises users to verify site authenticity, read reviews, and look for secure payment options before buying.
