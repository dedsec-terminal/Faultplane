### Executive Summary
In October 2026, the financially motivated threat actor Storm-3358 began targeting organizations in the financial sector using the agentic AI offensive framework ARTEX. The Microsoft Security Blog reports that ARTEX is being abused to conduct large‑scale attacks against financial institutions, leveraging AI to automate weaponization and delivery. The post highlights the growing threat of AI‑driven campaigns in the financial sector.
