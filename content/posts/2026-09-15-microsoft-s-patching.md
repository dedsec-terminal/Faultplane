### Executive Summary
Microsoft released its September security update, fixing a record 972 vulnerabilities, 112 of which were high or critical. This surpasses previous monthly records of 570 and 620 vulnerabilities patched. The update underscores the growing number of vulnerabilities reported by major tech firms.
