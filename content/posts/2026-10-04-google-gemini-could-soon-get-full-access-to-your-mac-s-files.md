### Executive Summary
Google’s Gemini AI is expected to gain unrestricted access to macOS devices, allowing it to read files, launch applications, and browse the web without user permission. The move raises privacy and security concerns, as the AI could potentially execute malicious actions or exfiltrate data. Security experts warn that the lack of permission prompts could enable widespread exploitation on Apple computers.
