### Executive Summary
The U.S. Department of Justice unsealed a 14‑count indictment charging 17 Iranian nationals, members of the Mabna Institute, with conducting a large‑scale cyber theft operation on behalf of Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). The indictment alleges the group targeted U.S. and foreign institutions, stealing sensitive data and intellectual property. The case underscores the U.S. effort to hold foreign actors accountable for cyber espionage and theft.
