### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs warns that AI assistants used to summarize inboxes can inadvertently turn ignored phishing emails into actionable tasks, as hidden instructions in the emails can be interpreted by the assistant. The article highlights the risk of AI assistants executing malicious commands embedded in phishing messages, potentially compromising systems.
