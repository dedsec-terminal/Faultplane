### Executive Summary
SANS Internet Storm Center reports a new ClickFix campaign that uses malicious links and social engineering to deliver malware to unsuspecting users. The threat actors exploit popular websites and email attachments to spread the payload, which can lead to credential theft and remote code execution. The report highlights detection indicators and recommends blocking known malicious domains and implementing user awareness training.
