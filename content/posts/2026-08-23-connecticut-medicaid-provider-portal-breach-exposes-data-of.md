### Executive Summary
Connecticut’s Medicaid provider portal was breached, exposing payment and claims data for about 41,000 HUSKY Health members. Gainwell Technologies, the state’s fiscal agent, detected the incident on June 25, 2026. The breach involved sensitive member information and could impact billing and reimbursement processes.
