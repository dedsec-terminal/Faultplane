### Executive Summary
The FBI has issued a warning that FortiBleed attacks are still active, targeting exposed Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN gateways. The exploits lock out legitimate administrators, preventing them from accessing the VPN. The attacks exploit a buffer‑overflow vulnerability in FortiGate’s SSL VPN, allowing attackers to gain unauthorized access and disrupt administrative control.
