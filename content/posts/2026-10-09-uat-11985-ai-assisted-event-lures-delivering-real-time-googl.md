### Executive Summary
Cisco Talos uncovered an APT spear‑phishing campaign targeting staff at Taiwanese research institutions. Attackers used AI‑generated event lures that mimicked legitimate public events and impersonated respected academic and policy bodies. The phishing emails leveraged real‑time Google AitM (Advanced Threat Intelligence) to deliver malicious payloads.
