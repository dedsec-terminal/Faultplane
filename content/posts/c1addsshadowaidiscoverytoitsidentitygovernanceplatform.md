C1 has introduced shadow AI discovery to its identity governance platform, aiming to eliminate security blind spots created by unauthorized AI agents, tools, and credentials. This feature automatically discovers and integrates AI-adjacent identities into the platform, ensuring that organizations can safely adopt AI. The shadow AI discovery works across cloud and endpoint surfaces, identifying unowned agents, over-permissioned service accounts, and unsanctioned AI tools.

The need for shadow AI discovery arises from the rapid growth of non-human identities (NHIs) within enterprises, particularly AI agents that authenticate and accumulate credentials independently. Existing security tools often miss these AI agents, leaving organizations vulnerable to breaches. According to IBM's Cost of a Data Breach Report 2025, one in five organizations experienced a breach due to shadow AI, with 97% of those breached lacking proper AI access controls.

With C1's shadow AI discovery, organizations can gain complete visibility into AI usage across their cloud and endpoint environments. The feature allows teams to assign owners, route requests, and certify AI access, making governance a lifecycle process rather than a one-time event. This enables security teams to protect against future attacks, scale AI adoption with confidence, and improve overall security by catching risks before they become incidents. As C1's CEO Alex Bovee notes, complete visibility into AI usage is essential for organizations to regain control and ensure safe AI adoption.
