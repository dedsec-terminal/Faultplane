### Executive Summary
Binance has denied claims of a new security breach by a threat actor using the 'ShinyHunters' handle on an underground cybercrime forum. The actor, believed to impersonate the ShinyHunters extortion gang, offered purported records of Binance customers for sale. Binance stated the post was a scam and that no breach occurred.
