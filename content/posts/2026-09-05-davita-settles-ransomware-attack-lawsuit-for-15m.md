### Executive Summary
DaVita, a nationwide kidney dialysis chain, agreed to pay $15 million to settle a class‑action lawsuit over a 2025 ransomware attack that exposed sensitive patient data. The breach was later leaked on the dark web, prompting the settlement to resolve claims from affected patients.
