### Executive Summary
A ransomware attack that merely locks corporate systems can trigger mandatory breach reporting under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, even if no data is exfiltrated. K7 CyberSecurity’s briefing (Nov 12 2026) explains that the DPDP defines a personal data breach as any incident that compromises personal data, including loss of availability. The report highlights that organizations must notify authorities and affected individuals when systems are compromised, regardless of data theft.
