### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers revealed a human‑operated phishing platform that masquerades as advertising portals for AI chatbots such as Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, Perplexity, Meta Muse, and Manus. The fake portals promise campaign optimization, spend audits, and business‑account connections, but instead harvest user credentials and MFA codes, enabling attackers to compromise accounts. The operation exemplifies sophisticated ad‑based credential theft targeting the growing AI chatbot ecosystem.
