### Executive Summary
Cybercriminals harvest credentials, session tokens, and API keys from compromised systems using infostealers such as Lumma Stealer or Vidar. The stolen data is used to create replayable AI tokens that bypass MFA, granting illicit access to AI services from providers like Google and Anthropic.
