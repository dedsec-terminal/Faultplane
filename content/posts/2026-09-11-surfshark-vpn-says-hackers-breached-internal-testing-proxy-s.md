### Executive Summary
Surfshark VPN revealed that a misconfigured internal test server was exposed to the internet, allowing attackers to access it. The breach involved one of the company’s proxy servers used for testing. Surfshark confirmed the incident, investigated the scope, and implemented additional security controls to prevent future exposure.
