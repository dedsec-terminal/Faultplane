### Executive Summary
The National Kidney Registry (NKR) is alleged to have been compromised by the DireWolf ransomware group, which surfaced in May 2025 and has targeted over 100 U.S. healthcare organizations. DireWolf’s attacks involve encrypting victims’ files and demanding ransom as part of a double‑extortion strategy. The group claims to be financially motivated.
