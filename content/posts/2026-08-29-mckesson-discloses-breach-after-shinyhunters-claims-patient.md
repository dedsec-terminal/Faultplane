### Executive Summary
McKesson, a major healthcare and pharmaceutical distributor, announced a cyber incident where attackers accessed third‑party applications and stole data. The extortion group ShinyHunters claims to have stolen 284 million patient records. McKesson is investigating the breach and has notified affected parties.
