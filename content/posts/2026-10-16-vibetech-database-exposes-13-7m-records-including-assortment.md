### Executive Summary
An unprotected database belonging to India-based AI software developer Vibetech was publicly accessible without authentication, exposing 13.7 million records that include credentials and data from prior breaches. The leak was discovered by researcher Jeremiah Fowler and reported by ExpressVPN and DataBreaches.net.
