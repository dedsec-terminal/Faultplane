### Executive Summary
A recent court decision awarded a state in a lawsuit against a telecom, underscoring that merely meeting technical requirements is insufficient for compliance with data‑breach notification laws. The ruling highlights how inadequate or “close‑enough” notifications can expose organizations to liability, and signals that regulators will scrutinize the adequacy of breach disclosures beyond technical fixes.
