### Executive Summary
The University of New Mexico Health Sciences Center experienced a ransomware attack that crippled its IT systems, leading to widespread outages. The breach halted patient care, postponed surgeries, and disrupted academic activities. The attack forced the institution to shut down critical services and prompted a response to mitigate the ransomware threat.
