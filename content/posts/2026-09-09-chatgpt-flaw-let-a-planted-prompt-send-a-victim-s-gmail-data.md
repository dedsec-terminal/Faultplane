### Executive Summary
Check Point Research reported that a single hidden instruction inserted into a ChatGPT conversation can covertly cause the model to read data from the user's connected Gmail account and forward it to a second ChatGPT account via a hidden channel, effectively enabling data exfiltration while the user receives normal responses.
