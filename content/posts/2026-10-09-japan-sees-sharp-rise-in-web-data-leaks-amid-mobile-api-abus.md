### Executive Summary
JPCERT/CC issued an alert on Oct 8 2026 detailing a surge of personal data leaks in Japanese organizations. Attackers exploited mobile app APIs and known software flaws, including vulnerabilities in Metabase. The alert does not name any specific attacker or victim organization.
