### Executive Summary

Medical technology company Boston Scientific is sending data breach notices to individuals affected by a cyberattack on the firm. According to a notification sample Boston Scientific shared with U.S. authorities, the hacker used social engineering in a voice phishing (vishing) attack to gain access to the company's cloud infrastructure. The attack exposed cloud-based applications and cloud-hosted data between May 7 and 1. The company became aware of the unauthorized access on May 12, and the investigation determined that a data breach occurred on November 5. Individuals in multiple U.S. states have been impacted, exposing personal details and medical information.
