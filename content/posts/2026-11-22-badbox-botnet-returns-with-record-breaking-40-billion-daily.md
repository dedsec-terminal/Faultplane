### Executive Summary
BADBOX botnet, targeting Android TV boxes, has resumed large‑scale ad‑fraud activity, generating an estimated 40 billion ad requests per day after last year’s disruptions. The malware spreads via compromised firmware and hijacks devices to serve fraudulent ads, inflating revenue for attackers. Operators use the botnet to bypass ad‑blockers and monetize traffic, posing a significant threat to advertisers and users.
