### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs uncovered counterfeit Microsoft-branded security scanners that fabricate threats, instruct users to uninstall legitimate antivirus software, and then redirect them to a refund scam. The deceptive tools mislead victims into believing their systems are compromised, prompting them to remove their real protection and fall prey to fraudulent offers.
