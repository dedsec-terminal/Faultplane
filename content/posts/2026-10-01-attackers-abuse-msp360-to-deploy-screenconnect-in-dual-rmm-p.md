### Executive Summary
Microsoft warned that attackers are distributing a legitimate MSP360 RMM installer under deceptive file names via phishing emails, meeting invites, PDFs, and software update prompts. Once executed, the installer grants remote management access and is used to deploy ScreenConnect, enabling attackers to control victim systems.
