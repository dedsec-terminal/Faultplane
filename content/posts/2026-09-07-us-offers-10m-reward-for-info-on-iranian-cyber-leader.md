### Executive Summary
The U.S. State Department has announced a $10 million reward for information leading to the arrest of Amir Yaryab, a senior Iranian official alleged to head the IRGC’s Cyber‑Electronic Command. Yaryab is accused of directing multiple Iranian hacking groups that have targeted critical U.S. infrastructure sectors, including defense, news, shipping, and energy. The reward seeks details on Yaryab’s whereabouts and activities.
