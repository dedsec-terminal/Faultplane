### Executive Summary
The Securelist report outlines the latest malware trends affecting Windows, macOS, and IoT devices during Q3 2026. It highlights the rise in targeted attacks, the shift toward more sophisticated payloads, and the increasing use of IoT as a foothold for lateral movement. Key statistics show a 30% increase in Windows malware incidents, a 25% rise on macOS, and a 40% surge in IoT-targeted threats, underscoring the need for stronger endpoint and network defenses.
