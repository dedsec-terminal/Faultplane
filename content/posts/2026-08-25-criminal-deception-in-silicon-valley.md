### Executive Summary
The paper analyzes court data on Silicon Valley entrepreneurs prosecuted for fraud (2000‑2023), showing that fraudsters use a façade strategy—constructing, performing, and protecting illusory appearances—to project high‑growth performance while hiding underperformance. It identifies three façade types (surface, reinforced, deep) tied to the gap between audience expectations and reality, and proposes deterrents such as expanded SEC surveillance, whistleblower programs, investor due diligence reforms, and entrepreneurship education.
