### Executive Summary
New York Attorney General Letitia James and three other state attorneys general secured a $10 million settlement and a corrective action plan from the Perry Johnson group of companies following a 2023 data breach that exposed sensitive personal information. The agreement includes remediation measures and ongoing monitoring to protect affected individuals.
