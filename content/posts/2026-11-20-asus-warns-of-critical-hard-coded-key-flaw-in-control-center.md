### Executive Summary
ASUS released Control Center Express v1.7.29 to patch three critical vulnerabilities that could allow remote attackers to gain administrative control over the management console and the devices it manages. The flaw involves a hard‑coded key that could be exploited to bypass authentication and execute privileged commands.
