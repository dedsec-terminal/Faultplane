### Executive Summary
The FBI, along with the US Secret Service and IRS Criminal Investigation, has officially linked the theft of more than $6 billion in virtual assets from Binance to the North Korea‑aligned hacking group TraderTraitor, also known as Jade Sleet, Pressure Chollima, PUKCHONG, Slow Pisces, and UNC4899. The indictment details the group’s use of sophisticated phishing, malware, and social engineering to siphon funds from Binance accounts worldwide.
