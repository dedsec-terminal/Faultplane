### Executive Summary
Kaspersky GReAT experts uncovered a new UEFI bootkit, BlackX, that exploits the Baton Drop vulnerability (CVE-2022-21894). BlackX closely resembles the earlier BlackLotus bootkit and was used by APT41 to target diplomatic organizations across Central Asia, demonstrating the group’s continued focus on high‑profile, politically sensitive targets.
