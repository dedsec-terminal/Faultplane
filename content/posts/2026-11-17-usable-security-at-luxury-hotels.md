### Executive Summary
A case study shows attackers sent a fake booking request to a luxury hotel’s reservations inbox, copied legitimate refund requests, and submitted fraudulent ones for approval. The incident highlights that generic advice like “just don’t click on links” fails in environments where staff must respond to unknown emails, and that a fragmented security model among owner, operator, and brand creates accountability gaps, leading to staff blame.
