### Executive Summary
A new Utah law strengthens student privacy protections after Brigham Young University researchers discovered that many K‑12 educational technology applications were collecting and sharing student data without proper safeguards. The study, presented to the Utah State Board of Education in August 2025, highlighted gaps in vendor compliance with privacy obligations. In response, lawmakers enacted legislation tightening data collection limits, requiring clearer consent, and imposing stricter oversight on EdTech vendors.
