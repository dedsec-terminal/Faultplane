### Executive Summary
A threat actor compromised the TeamUpdraft website, stealing UpdraftCentral connection keys. These keys allow remote command execution on any WordPress site connected to UpdraftCentral, effectively creating a backdoor. The breach could enable attackers to control or exfiltrate data from affected sites.
