### Executive Summary
A critical PHP core flaw has been discovered that can allow attackers to take full control of affected servers. Nearly 70% of websites are vulnerable, and working exploits are already in use, causing widespread 404 and 'Page Not Found' errors. Site owners must update PHP immediately to mitigate the risk.
