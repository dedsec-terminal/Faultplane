### Executive Summary
SonicWall announced it will not patch two vulnerabilities affecting end‑of‑life Global Management System (GMS) versions 9.5.2 and earlier. The flaws, including CVE‑2026‑109593 (CVSS 9.1), allow unauthenticated attackers with network access to read internal GMS files and potentially alter database records. Users of these legacy systems are advised to migrate to supported versions or mitigate through network segmentation.
