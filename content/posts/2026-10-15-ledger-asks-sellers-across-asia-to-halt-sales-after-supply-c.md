### Executive Summary
Ledger has instructed resellers and distributors in Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia, and Hong Kong to stop selling its hardware wallets and remove them from shelves while it investigates reports of device tampering linked to a supply‑chain attack. The company is working with authorities and security teams to assess the extent of the compromise and protect users.
