### Executive Summary
Microsoft Threat Intelligence reports that threat actors employed the agentic offensive security framework ARTEX to scan GitHub for exposed OAuth application secrets. By compromising these OAuth apps, attackers gained authenticated access to the associated tenants and performed a range of malicious actions within the victim organizations.
