### Executive Summary
Norway's Data Protection Authority fined Microsoft-owned advertising company Xandr NOK 92 million ($9.6 million) for unlawfully processing and sharing sensitive Grindr user data in the country. The fine follows findings that Xandr collected and shared personal information without users’ consent, violating Norway’s privacy laws and the EU’s GDPR principles.
