### Executive Summary
The Technical University of Denmark (DTU) reported that hackers accessed its identity and access management system, potentially exposing personal data of up to 200,000 users. The breach involved downloading a large amount of data, including user credentials and other sensitive information. DTU is investigating the incident and has notified relevant authorities.
