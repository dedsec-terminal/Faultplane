### Executive Summary
Microsoft Security reports that the ransomware affiliate Storm-2570 consistently applies the same post‑compromise tools and techniques across multiple ransomware families—Qilin, DragonForce, Anubis, and BERT. The blog outlines the tradecraft, including shared command‑and‑control infrastructure, lateral movement tactics, and data exfiltration patterns, and offers defenders actionable guidance to detect and disrupt the activity before ransomware is deployed.
