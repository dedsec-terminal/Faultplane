### Executive Summary
eMudhra, an Indian certificate authority, revoked eight additional intermediate CA certificates after an internal investigation revealed improper handling of private keys during the certificate‑generation process. The revocation follows a prior incident where eight certificates were also revoked due to a private‑key mishap. The action underscores the risks of private‑key mismanagement and the importance of secure key handling practices.
