### Executive Summary
A new mobile‑focused scam mimics an Apple Pay notification, using a fake alert and browser tricks to trick users into calling a fraudulent support line. The attacker displays a counterfeit Apple Pay charge, then redirects the victim to a malicious website that prompts them to dial a phone number. Once on the line, scammers claim to resolve the issue, but instead attempt to harvest personal data or install malware.
