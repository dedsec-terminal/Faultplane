### Executive Summary
CISA added two new vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after attackers chained three flaws in Ivanti Connect Secure (ICS) to achieve unauthenticated root access. The flaws include CVE-2026-111357 (CVSS 8.8) and two others, enabling attackers to bypass authentication and gain full system control. Active exploitation observed in the wild.
