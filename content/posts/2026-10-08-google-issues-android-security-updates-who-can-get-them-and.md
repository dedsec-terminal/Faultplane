### Executive Summary
Google released new Android security updates that patch several vulnerabilities, but many users will not receive them immediately. The rollout depends on device compatibility, carrier agreements, and OEM update schedules. Users can check for updates in Settings > System > Advanced > System update, and those on supported devices will receive the patch within a few weeks.
