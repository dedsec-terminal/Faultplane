### Executive Summary
Paper ballots remain the backbone of U.S. elections because they provide proven chain‑of‑custody and auditability that electronic systems cannot fully match. Cryptographic protocols such as Helios and ElectionGuard can keep votes secret and enable verifiable tallies, but they rely on digital identities and are vulnerable to client or server compromise. The National Academy of Sciences warns that online voting cannot guarantee secrecy, integrity, or verifiability, so paper ballots are still essential for public elections.
