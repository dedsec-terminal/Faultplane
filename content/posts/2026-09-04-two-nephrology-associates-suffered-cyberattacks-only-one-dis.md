### Executive Summary
Two separate entities named Nephrology Associates were hit by cyberattacks in 2024. One incident, reported on March 7 in Kansas, was publicly disclosed, while the other remains unreported. The attacks were unrelated, despite the shared name, and highlight the importance of accurate attribution in breach reporting.
