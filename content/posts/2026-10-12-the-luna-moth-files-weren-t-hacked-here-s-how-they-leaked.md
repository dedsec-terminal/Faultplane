### Executive Summary
The Silent Ransom Group denied a breach of the Luna Moth Files, yet the data surfaced on an anonymous onion site. DataBreaches.net investigates the leak’s origin, revealing that the files were likely exfiltrated by the group itself. The article details how the files were uploaded, the lack of attribution, and the implications for data security. It clarifies that the files were not hacked but deliberately released, exposing the group’s operational methods.
