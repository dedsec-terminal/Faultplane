### Executive Summary
Microsoft Security reports that threat actors use passkey-themed social engineering to trick users into revealing MFA credentials, establishing persistence, and then abuse Microsoft Graph for reconnaissance. They target SharePoint, OneDrive, and email data, enabling broader cloud compromise. The post outlines detection signals and mitigation steps.
