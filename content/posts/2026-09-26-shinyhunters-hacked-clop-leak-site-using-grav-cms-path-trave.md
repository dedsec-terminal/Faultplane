### Executive Summary
ShinyHunters exploited an unauthenticated path‑traversal flaw in Grav CMS to deface the Clop ransomware gang’s data‑leak site. The attack forced Clop to move the site to a new Tor address. The vulnerability, present in unpatched Grav installations, allowed attackers to read arbitrary files and modify site content.
