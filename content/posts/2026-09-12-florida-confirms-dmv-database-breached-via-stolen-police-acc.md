### Executive Summary
The Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV) confirmed that its DAVID driver database was compromised. Attackers accessed the system using credentials belonging to an employee of a local police department. The breach exposed personal data of Florida drivers, prompting the agency to investigate the incident and enhance security measures.
