Slop or Not is an AI detection app for iPhone and Mac that uses on-device AI models to identify AI-generated content, including images and text. The app runs entirely offline, requiring no account or internet connection, and utilizes the Apple Neural Engine to power its detection capabilities. With the increasing prevalence of AI-generated scams, including deepfakes and personalized phishing attacks, Slop or Not aims to help users distinguish between authentic and fake content.

The app includes a quiz feature that tests users' ability to identify AI-generated images, as well as a probability score that indicates the likelihood of an image being AI-generated. Slop or Not also checks for Google's invisible SynthID watermark, which can verify the authenticity of compatible images. In testing, the app was able to correctly identify AI-generated images with varying degrees of confidence, although some images fell into a gray area and were not definitively classified. The app also correctly identified genuine photographs as real.

Slop or Not can be a useful tool for individuals who regularly encounter AI-generated content, including students, educators, journalists, and businesses. The app can help verify the authenticity of images and text, reducing the risk of scams and misinformation. However, it is essential to note that AI detection is not an exact science, and no tool can guarantee perfect accuracy. Slop or Not should be used in conjunction with critical thinking and human judgment to support informed decisions. By providing an additional layer of protection against AI-generated content, Slop or Not can help users make more informed decisions and stay safe online.
