### Executive Summary
Healthcare provider AdaptHealth disclosed that a July cyberattack exposed personal data of 4.1 million individuals. The breach was attributed to the ShinyHunters threat group. The incident involved unauthorized access to patient records, including names, addresses, dates of birth, and medical information. AdaptHealth is investigating the scope and impact of the exposure.
