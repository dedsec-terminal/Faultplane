### Executive Summary
Researchers discovered that a malicious app installed on a OnePlus 15 running OxygenOS can gain root access without requesting any permissions. By chaining two unpatched vulnerabilities in OnePlus’s software, the attacker can obtain full system control. The flaws also affect other OnePlus devices and OPPO phones, but the company has not yet released a fix.
