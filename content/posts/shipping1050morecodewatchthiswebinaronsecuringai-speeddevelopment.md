The increasing use of AI in development teams has led to a significant surge in code production, with output jumping 10 to 50 times faster. However, this rapid development pace puts a strain on security teams, who must review vulnerabilities, manage dependencies, and prioritize fixes at a much slower "human speed." As a result, security can become a bottleneck, and the risk of losing control over what gets shipped increases.

The traditional application security cycle, where developers write code, scanners identify problems, and security teams prioritize and fix issues, is no longer sufficient. With AI-driven development, the number of components, dependencies, and findings to manage grows exponentially, creating a larger backlog. Moreover, the same AI models that aid developers are also available to attackers, putting security teams under pressure from both sides. The core challenge is to move at AI speed without accepting AI-speed risk, requiring a new operating model for security.

The webinar "The True Cost of Building at Machine Speed" addresses this challenge by exploring the limitations of traditional vulnerability management processes and the need for secure-by-default development. It discusses how to build controls that can keep pace with AI adoption and examines the governance side of AI-assisted development, including risk ownership and exposure. The webinar provides a practical framework for securing AI-driven development, enabling security teams to work at the same speed as development teams and ensuring that security controls are designed for the current development landscape, rather than relying on outdated methods.
