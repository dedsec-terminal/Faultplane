### Executive Summary
The Department of Defense confirmed refrigeration outages at 14 military commissaries across the continental U.S., affecting food storage and supply chains. While the Pentagon acknowledges the problem, it has not determined whether the disruptions were caused by a cyberattack. The incidents have raised concerns about operational security and supply reliability for service members.
