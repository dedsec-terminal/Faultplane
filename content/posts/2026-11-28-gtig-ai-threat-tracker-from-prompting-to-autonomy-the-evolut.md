### Executive Summary

Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) reported that in Q2 2026 adversaries moved from simple prompting to fully autonomous AI workflows, enabling rapid credential‑harvesting campaigns that can be planned, built, and executed in under six hours. The group observed attackers compromising cloud resources, targeting AI supply chains by tricking AI coding assistants and LLM security scanners, and stealing proprietary AI models, source code, and API credentials. These activities threaten enterprise AI assets—including model weights, cloud compute quotas, and intellectual property—through espionage, extortion, and resource theft. GTIG’s defensive guidance emphasizes proactive model‑level safeguards, targeted containment protocols, and an autonomous Google AI Threat Defense architecture designed to disrupt malicious projects and accounts while hardening models against misuse.
