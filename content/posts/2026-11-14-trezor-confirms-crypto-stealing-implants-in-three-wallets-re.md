### Executive Summary
Trezor confirmed that three Safe 5 hardware wallets sold by Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis contained malicious hardware implants that captured wallet recovery phrases and exfiltrated them via cellular networks to attackers. The company issued a firmware update to remove the implants and advised users to verify device authenticity and update firmware. The incident highlights the risk of supply‑chain attacks on crypto hardware wallets.
