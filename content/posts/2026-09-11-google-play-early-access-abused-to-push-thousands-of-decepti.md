### Executive Summary
Bad actors exploit Google Play’s Early Access program to distribute deceptive Android apps that promise money, rewards, casino winnings, and premium content. These apps, not yet released on the official marketplace, trick users into downloading them under false pretenses, potentially exposing them to malware or phishing. The abuse highlights the need for stricter vetting of Early Access submissions.
