### Executive Summary
Revolut was not hacked in the traditional sense; instead, an individual posing as a legitimate government official requested customer data via email. The bank complied, handing over the information. The incident highlights that data breaches can occur through social engineering and raises questions about how insurers classify and respond to such events.
