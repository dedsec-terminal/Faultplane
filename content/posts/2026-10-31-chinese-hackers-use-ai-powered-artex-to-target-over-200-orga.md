### Executive Summary
Chinese state-sponsored threat actors have been observed deploying a Go-based AI‑powered penetration testing framework called ARTEX to conduct simultaneous attacks against high‑value targets. The campaign, attributed by CrowdStrike to the China‑based adversary group Vapor Panda (formerly VexatiousTemper), has impacted more than 200 organizations worldwide.
