### Executive Summary

Drupal has identified a new highly critical vulnerability in its core software, CVE‑2026‑136716, that has already been exploited in the wild. The flaw stems from improper checks for unusual or exceptional conditions, allowing attackers to potentially take over affected sites. The issue is related to the earlier CVE‑2026‑132165 (Fail2Install) and addresses remaining vulnerable installation states.

### Original Overview

Drupal has warned of a new "highly critical" security flaw impacting Drupal core that it said has been exploited in the wild. The vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-136716 (CVSS score: N/A), has been described as a case of improper checking for unusual or exceptional conditions. It also relates to CVE-2026-132165 aka Fail2Install in that it addresses remaining vulnerable installation states
