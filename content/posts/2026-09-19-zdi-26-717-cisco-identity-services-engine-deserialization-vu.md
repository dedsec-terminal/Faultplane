### Executive Summary
A deserialization flaw in Cisco Identity Services Engine’s AlarmMessageDiskQueue component allows authenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary code. The vulnerability, rated CVSS 7.2, is identified as CVE-2026-20211 and was disclosed by the Zero Day Initiative.

This vulnerability allows remote attackers to execute arbitrary code on affected installations of Cisco Identity Services Engine. Authentication is required to exploit this vulnerability. The ZDI has assigned a CVSS rating of 7.2. The following CVEs are assigned: CVE-2026-20211.
