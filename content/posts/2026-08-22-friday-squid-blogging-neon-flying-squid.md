### Executive Summary
Researchers observed a shoal of about 100 neon flying squid (Ommastrephes bartramii) unexpectedly rise from the Pacific Ocean near Tokyo and glide in formation for roughly 30 metres. This is the first photographic evidence of the species’ aerial gliding, a behavior previously only rumored. The sighting offers new insight into the flight capabilities of the 20‑species flying squid family.
