### Executive Summary
A new variant of a known scam uses a fake CAPTCHA interface to trick users into downloading and executing malicious software. Attackers embed the CAPTCHA prompt in a deceptive webpage or email, convincing victims that they must solve a CAPTCHA to proceed. Once the user clicks the link or submits the form, a malware payload is delivered, often masquerading as a legitimate installer. The technique exploits users’ trust in CAPTCHAs and the urgency of completing a task.
