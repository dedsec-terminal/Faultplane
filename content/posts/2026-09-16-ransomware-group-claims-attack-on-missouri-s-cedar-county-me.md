### Executive Summary
A ransomware group claimed responsibility for an attack that caused an IT outage at Cedar County Memorial Hospital in El Dorado Springs, Missouri, shutting down its electronic health record, patient portal, internet access, and diagnostic imaging services on Aug. 14. The outage forced the emergency department to partially divert trauma and critical patients.
