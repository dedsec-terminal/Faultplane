Mimecast has introduced two new capabilities to enhance cybersecurity: Agent Risk Center and Managed Threat Response. The Agent Risk Center is a beta capability that allows security teams to discover, monitor, and govern AI agents operating in their environment. This is in response to the growing use of AI tools in organizations, with 98% of companies already having unsanctioned AI tools in use. The Agent Risk Center provides a single point of control to manage AI agents, including a unified dashboard, AI rulebook, and response controls.

The Agent Risk Center aims to address the limited visibility security teams have into AI agent behavior, permissions, and data access. With this capability, security teams can apply the same risk lens to AI agents as they do to human insiders. The beta version is currently available as a free, opt-in capability for active Incydr subscription customers, with general availability planned for January 2027. According to Rob Juncker, Chief Technology & Product Officer at Mimecast, "AI agents are being deployed faster than security teams can see them," and the Agent Risk Center changes that by providing a single place to see and act on AI agent activity.

Mimecast has also introduced Managed Threat Response, a redesigned 24/7 managed service that combines AI-accelerated triage with analyst-confirmed remediation. This service addresses the problem of uninvestigated security alerts, with 42% of alerts going entirely uninvestigated. Managed Threat Response uses AI to triage every reported email, and then human analysts action confirmed threats, blocking senders or domains, removing campaigns, and updating detection logic. The service provides continuous improvement in detection logic as confirmed threats are identified across Mimecast's customer base, with no additional console, model tuning, or analyst headcount required.
