### Executive Summary
On Sept 2 2026, Delaware Gov signed HB 380 and HB 381. HB 380 amends the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act (DPDPA) enacted 2023, effective Jan 1 2025, adding new privacy obligations. HB 381 amends the state’s computer security breach notification law, updating notice requirements and timelines.
