### Executive Summary
BrickLink, the online marketplace for LEGO parts, went offline after detecting unusual activity on its platform. The company has not confirmed whether customer data was compromised, but users are advised to monitor accounts and be cautious of potential phishing attempts. The incident highlights the importance of monitoring for suspicious activity on e‑commerce sites.
