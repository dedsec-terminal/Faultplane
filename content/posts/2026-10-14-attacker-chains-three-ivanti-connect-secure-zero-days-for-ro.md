### Executive Summary
An attacker chained three zero‑day vulnerabilities in Ivanti Connect Secure, a VPN appliance, to execute commands as root without authentication. Security firm Volexity reported the exploit on Oct. 13. Ivanti has released patches for two of the flaws, urging customers to act immediately, but a fix for the third zero‑day remains pending.
