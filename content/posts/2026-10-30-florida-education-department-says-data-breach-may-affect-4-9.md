### Executive Summary
The Florida Department of Education disclosed that unauthorized criminal access to its Student Financial Aid website on Oct. 19 may have exposed personal data—including Social Security numbers—of roughly 4.9 million individuals. The breach prompted an immediate cybersecurity response and investigation into the extent of the compromise.
