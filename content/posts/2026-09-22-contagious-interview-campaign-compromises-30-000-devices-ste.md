### Executive Summary
North Korean threat actors behind the Contagious Interview campaign have compromised at least 30,000 devices in over 100 countries, targeting web designers, engineers, and crypto specialists. The operation siphoned funds or credentials from more than 7,000 cryptocurrency wallets, totaling $10.71 million in stolen assets.
