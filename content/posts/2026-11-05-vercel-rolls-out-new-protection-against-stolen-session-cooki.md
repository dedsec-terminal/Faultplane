### Executive Summary
Vercel is gradually deploying Device Bound Session Credentials (DBSC) across its platform to mitigate stolen session cookie attacks, a common vector for information stealer malware. DBSC ties session tokens to device-specific attributes, preventing attackers from reusing captured cookies on other devices. The rollout is part of Vercel’s broader effort to strengthen web application security.
