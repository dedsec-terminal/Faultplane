### Executive Summary
WordPress plugins and themes—WPMU DEV Dashboard, Avada, TranslatePress, Pods, and GiveWP—contain critical flaws that enable authentication bypass, account takeover, and arbitrary code execution. The most severe, CVE‑2026‑76581, scores 9.8 on CVSS. These vulnerabilities allow attackers to take full control of affected sites.
