### Executive Summary
Brave Browser version 1.94 introduces Email Aliases, a feature that lets users create disposable email addresses for new service sign‑ups. The aliases forward mail to the user’s real inbox while masking the original address, reducing tracking and spam. The update is part of Brave’s privacy‑focused enhancements and is available on all platforms.
