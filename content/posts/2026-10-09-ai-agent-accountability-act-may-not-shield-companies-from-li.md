### Executive Summary
U.S. Senators Josh Hawley and Chris Murphy propose the AI Agent Accountability Act, aiming to hold AI‑agent operators and developers liable for civil or criminal wrongdoing. The bill argues that claiming “It wasn’t me, it was the AI agent” does not absolve companies from responsibility. The legislation seeks to clarify liability and prevent misuse of AI agents.
