### Executive Summary
Google disclosed that threat actors now deploy autonomous multi‑agent AI frameworks—such as ARTEX, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI, and PentAGI—to automatically generate and execute exploits from software patches. These systems leverage large language models to orchestrate multi‑stage intrusions at higher operational velocity, marking a shift from manual prompting to fully autonomous offensive capabilities.
