### Executive Summary
Unit 42’s analysis of the Aeternum botnet loader reveals a novel use of the Polygon blockchain to host decentralized command‑and‑control (C2) infrastructure. The loader pulls instructions from smart contracts, enabling the botnet to evade traditional detection and maintain persistence. Payloads are executed directly from the blockchain, allowing the threat actor to update malware, distribute new modules, and orchestrate attacks without relying on conventional servers.
