### Executive Summary
The Lock and Code podcast episode features Kim Sutherland discussing how fraudsters are exploiting loyalty points to finance their vacations. She explains the mechanics of the scheme, the types of loyalty programs most at risk, and offers practical steps for consumers to protect their accounts and detect suspicious activity. The episode emphasizes the importance of monitoring points balances, using unique passwords, and enabling two‑factor authentication.
