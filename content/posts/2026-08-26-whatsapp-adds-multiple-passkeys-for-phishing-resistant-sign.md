### Executive Summary
Meta announced that WhatsApp will now support multiple passkeys per account, enabling users on iOS and Android to sign in with phishing‑resistant credentials. The feature, first rolled out on Android in October 2023, is expected to protect over 1 billion users who already use passkeys for WhatsApp login.
