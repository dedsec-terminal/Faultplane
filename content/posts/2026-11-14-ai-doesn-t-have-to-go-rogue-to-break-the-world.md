### Executive Summary
Bruce Schneier’s DEF CON 34 keynote essay explores three facets of AI hacking: (1) humans hacking AI systems, (2) humans using AI to attack other systems, and (3) autonomous AIs hacking systems without malicious intent. He argues the latter two are growing more prevalent as AI improves at autonomous problem‑solving, even without major breakthroughs. The essay highlights emerging trends that could destabilize security, urging vigilance as AI capabilities expand.
