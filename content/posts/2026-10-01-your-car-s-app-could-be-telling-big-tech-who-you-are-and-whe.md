### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs reports that many modern car infotainment apps transmit personal data—including location, identity, and health‑related information—to third‑party trackers. The data is shared via embedded SDKs and telemetry, allowing large tech firms to build detailed user profiles. The study highlights the privacy risks of connected vehicles and calls for stricter data‑handling controls.
