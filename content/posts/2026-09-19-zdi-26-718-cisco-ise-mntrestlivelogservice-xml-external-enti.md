### Executive Summary
A remote information disclosure vulnerability exists in Cisco Identity Services Engine's MnTRESTLivelogService due to XML External Entity (XXE) processing. Authentication is required to exploit the flaw, which can reveal sensitive data. The vulnerability is assigned CVE-2026-20235 and has a CVSS score of 4.9.
