### Executive Summary
McKesson, a major U.S. healthcare services provider, confirmed a cyber incident after the hacking group ShinyHunters announced it had stolen hundreds of millions of patient records. The breach reportedly involved sensitive personal and medical data, raising concerns about the security of healthcare data. McKesson has not yet disclosed the full extent of the compromise or the specific data accessed.
