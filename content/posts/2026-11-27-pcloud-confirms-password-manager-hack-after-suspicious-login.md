### Executive Summary

The pCloud Pass password manager suffered a cyberattack, with users reporting that unknown devices had connected to their accounts even with two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled. The incident was first reported by numerous pCloud customers who began receiving email notifications informing them that a new device had connected to their pCloud Pass accounts. In two Reddit threads , customers worldwide reported receiving notifications warning that an unfamiliar device named "pd" connected to their account from the IP address 45.131.244.7 in Luxembourg, which users say belongs to pCloud's own infrastructure.
