F5 has released enhancements to its AI Gateway, integrating the solution into the F5 AI Security Platform. The updated gateway enforces policies on every AI request, providing enterprises with a unified control plane to govern how AI models, agents, and tools are accessed and used. The changes aim to optimize the economics of AI at scale while improving governance and security for organizations that have moved beyond experimentation.
