### Executive Summary
Breeze Comet (formerly UNC5669) is a financially motivated threat actor that has targeted Brazilian financial services, retail, and e‑commerce organizations since 2024. According to Google Threat Intelligence Group and Mandiant, the group specializes in manipulating payment systems and banking software in Brazil to execute fraudulent transfers. The actor has carried out hundreds of illicit transactions through compromised payment platforms.
