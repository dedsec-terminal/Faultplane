### Executive Summary
A critical out‑of‑bounds write flaw in Cisco Snort’s sf_unfold_header component allows remote attackers to execute arbitrary code without authentication. The vulnerability carries a CVSS score of 9.8 and is identified as CVE-2026-20043.
