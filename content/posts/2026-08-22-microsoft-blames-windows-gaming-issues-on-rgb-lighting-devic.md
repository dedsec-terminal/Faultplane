### Executive Summary
Microsoft attributes recent Windows 2026 update crashes in games to RGB lighting peripherals. The August 2026 update reportedly caused games to crash or fail to launch when RGB devices are connected. Microsoft advises disabling RGB lighting or updating device firmware to mitigate the problem.
