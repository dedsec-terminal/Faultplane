### Executive Summary

Flight Centre Travel Group announced on November 26 that it was investigating a cyber incident involving a third‑party accommodation booking system used by several of its leisure travel brands. The company said it detected unusual activity on the platform and that the breach could have exposed a significant number of customer records, including passport details. Flight Centre is currently investigating the scope of the exposure and will notify affected customers as appropriate. No specific defensive guidance was provided in the announcement.
