### Executive Summary
Japanese manufacturer Iris Ohyama revealed that personal data of roughly 7.7 million customers may have been exposed after unauthorized access to a third‑party cloud service it used for its Iris Plaza shopping platform. The breach involved personal information such as names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses. Iris Ohyama has notified affected individuals and is investigating the incident.
