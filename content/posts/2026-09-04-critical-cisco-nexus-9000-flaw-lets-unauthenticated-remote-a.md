### Executive Summary
Cisco released patches for a critical flaw (CVE-2026-20212, CVSS 9.8) affecting Silicon One-based Nexus 9000 switches. The vulnerability allows unauthenticated remote attackers to execute code with root privileges. An IOS XR hardening release bundles 7 umbrella CVEs, 2 rated 9.8, with no workaround for any IOS XR version.
