Here are three concise paragraphs summarizing the article:

This week's ThreatsDay report highlights various cybersecurity threats, including the Odysseus RCE, Samsung One-Click Takeover, and iCloud Backdoor Fight, among 27 other stories. The report notes that many of these threats rely on "cheap leverage" such as exposed servers, recycled bugs, and poisoned agent instructions. These threats often exploit trust in ordinary systems, allowing attackers to gain access and control.

Several specific threats are highlighted, including a China-linked telecom risk, where Chinese telecom firms operating in the US may be used to facilitate cyber operations against the US. Additionally, a ClickOnce phishing chain has been discovered, where threat actors use ClickOnce application files delivered via phishing PDF documents to deliver Rust-based backdoors. An npm supply chain attack has also been reported, where malicious packages are being used to deliver payloads to unsuspecting users.

Other notable threats include the use of AI-powered cyber attacks, where threat actors are using AI agents to break into third-party networks. A new version of the XCSSET malware is also targeting macOS users, with advanced architecture and detection evasion capabilities. Furthermore, vulnerabilities in Samsung devices have been discovered, which could allow for remote system-level compromise with just one click. These threats demonstrate the evolving nature of cybersecurity risks and the importance of staying informed and vigilant to protect against them.
