### Executive Summary
Trezor has confirmed credible reports of a suspected hardware implant designed to steal cryptocurrency from its Safe 5 wallets sold by reseller CryptoBilis in Southeast Asia. The implant could intercept private keys during transactions. Trezor urges users to check firmware, update, and contact support. The incident highlights supply‑chain risks for hardware wallets.
