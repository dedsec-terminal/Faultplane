Here are 3 concise paragraphs summarizing the new infosec products of the week:

Several companies have released new infosec products this week, including ScienceLogic, Searchlight Cyber, A10 Networks, and SelectHub. ScienceLogic has announced Skylar AI 2.5, which expands secure deployment options and introduces enhancements to strengthen AI performance and operational intelligence. This release improves AI accuracy, platform performance, and user experience across the ScienceLogic AI Platform.

Searchlight Cyber has launched its Preemptive Threat Exposure Management (PTEM) platform, which combines exposure visibility with real-world attacker intelligence to help organizations prioritize and reduce vulnerabilities. The platform provides continuous exposure visibility, attack surface discovery, and exploitability validation, as well as intelligence on threat actors' discussions, developments, and targets. A10 Networks has also introduced the A10 AI Gateway, a centralized control plane that secures and manages enterprise AI by providing unified routing, cost management, and governance.

SelectHub has announced the launch of DataGrout, an LLM inference optimization platform and AI governance solution for enterprises. DataGrout aims to reduce token usage for AI workflows and equip IT and FinOps leadership with a policy-driven, auditable system to monitor company-wide AI utilization. These new products aim to enhance security, governance, and management of AI and IT operations, providing organizations with more effective tools to protect themselves from threats and optimize their AI usage.
