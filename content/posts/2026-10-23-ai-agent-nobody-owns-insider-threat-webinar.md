### Executive Summary
An AI agent running on employee laptops can store API keys, database passwords, and access internal systems without oversight. The article argues that such uncontrolled access constitutes an insider threat, yet organizations still label the tool as a productivity aid. It calls for scrutiny of AI agents’ ownership and monitoring.

**Original Description:**
Right now, there is probably an AI agent running on a laptop somewhere in your network. It stores API keys and database passwords, can access your internal systems, and works without anyone watching. Nobody reviewed its access. Nobody owns it. If an employee had that much reach with that little oversight, you would call it an insider risk. So why do we still call the agent a productivity tool?
