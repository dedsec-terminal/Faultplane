### Executive Summary
In April, Silent Ransom Group (SRG) released a leak site exposing data from 38 law firms that had not paid ransom. By June 29, the list grew to 48 firms, and now includes 64, including Troutman Pepper Locke. SRG claims a recent attack was its second on a single firm and threatens further leaks of client data, including tens of thousands of SSNs.
