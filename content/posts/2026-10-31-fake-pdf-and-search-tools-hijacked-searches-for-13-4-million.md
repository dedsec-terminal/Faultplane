### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs uncovered 319 malicious Chrome extensions that redirected users’ search queries to attacker‑controlled servers, enabling the operators to monitor and log search terms. The compromised extensions, disguised as PDF and search tools, affected approximately 13.4 million users, exposing their search activity to the attackers.
