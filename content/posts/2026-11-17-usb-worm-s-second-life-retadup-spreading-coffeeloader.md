### Executive Summary
A new variant of the CoffeeLoader banking trojan is now being distributed via the Retadup USB worm, which was previously dismantled by law enforcement in 2019. The attackers have repurposed Retadup’s infrastructure to deliver the latest CoffeeLoader payload, demonstrating how dormant malware can be revived for new campaigns.
