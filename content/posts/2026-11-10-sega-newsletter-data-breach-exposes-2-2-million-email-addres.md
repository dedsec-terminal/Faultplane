### Executive Summary
Japanese gaming company SEGA announced that a data breach at its third‑party newsletter provider, ARARA, exposed the email addresses of 2.2 million subscribers. The breach was discovered after a security audit and the company is working with ARARA to mitigate the impact. No other personal data was reported as compromised.
