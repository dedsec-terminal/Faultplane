Adobe has released updates to address multiple critical security vulnerabilities in ColdFusion, Commerce, and Campaign Classic. The most severe flaws have CVSS scores of 10.0, indicating a high risk of exploitation. These vulnerabilities could lead to arbitrary code execution, privilege escalation, and denial-of-service attacks if successfully exploited.

The updates fix seven vulnerabilities, including operating system command injection, eval injection, and incorrect authorization flaws. The affected products and their corresponding fixes are: ColdFusion (2025.0.12 and 2023.0.23), Commerce, and Campaign Classic (ACC v7 7.4.4 build 9400). The updates for ColdFusion and Campaign Classic have a Priority 1 rating, indicating a higher risk of being targeted by malicious cyber attacks.

Administrators are recommended to install the updates as soon as possible, preferably within 72 hours, as the vulnerabilities have a high risk of exploitation. Although there is no evidence of these flaws being exploited in the wild, prompt action is necessary to prevent potential attacks. It's worth noting that Adobe-hosted instances of Campaign Classic have already been remediated and require no customer action, while the updates only apply to fully on-premise deployments and on-premise components of hybrid deployments.
