### Executive Summary
On September 6, attackers stole ~3,998 BTC from the Liquid sidechain via an Elements bug. The next day, 3,400 BTC were returned to the network, leaving 598.5 BTC unreturned. Liquid remains paused, preventing token redemption. The incident highlights vulnerabilities in sidechain implementations and the ongoing risk to crypto custodians.
