### Executive Summary
Microsoft Threat Intelligence reports a high‑volume phishing campaign that leverages Microsoft 365 outage‑themed lures to trick organizations across various industry sectors worldwide. The campaign uses deceptive emails mimicking outage notifications to steal credentials and deliver malware.
