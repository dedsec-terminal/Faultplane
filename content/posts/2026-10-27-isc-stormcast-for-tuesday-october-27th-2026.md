### Executive Summary
The SANS Internet Storm Center released its daily stormcast for Tuesday, October 27, 2026, summarizing the latest global threat landscape. The report highlighted increased phishing campaigns targeting financial institutions, a surge in ransomware activity in the Asia‑Pacific region, and a new exploit for a widely used web server. It also noted a spike in DNS tunneling traffic and provided actionable indicators of compromise, such as malicious IP addresses and domain names. The ISC team urged organizations to review their security posture and apply relevant patches.
