### Executive Summary
Michael “Miki” Bar, a 43‑year‑old former Chief Information Security Officer of the Hamat Group, was arrested and indicted by Israel’s State Attorney’s Office for remotely accessing cameras, stealing passwords, and infiltrating 26 companies. The indictment alleges Bar used his former role to gain unauthorized access, compromising sensitive data across multiple firms. The Hamat Group denies any connection to the charges.
