### Executive Summary
SCKit, a threat actor, leveraged the legitimate remote‑access tool ScreenConnect to install unauthorized remote‑access software on victim systems. The malware then targets active technician sessions, allowing attackers to hijack privileged accounts and exfiltrate data. The post outlines the attack chain, recommends mitigations such as restricting ScreenConnect usage and monitoring for anomalous remote‑access activity, and demonstrates how Microsoft Defender XDR can hunt for related indicators.
