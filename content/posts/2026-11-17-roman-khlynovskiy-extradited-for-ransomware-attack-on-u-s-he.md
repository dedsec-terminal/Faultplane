### Executive Summary
Kazakh national Roman Khlynovskiy, 43, was extradited from Italy to the U.S. to face federal charges for his alleged role in a ransomware and extortion conspiracy that targeted U.S. hospitals, healthcare entities, and schools. Prosecutors say the plot was part of the 8‑Digits Team, one of the largest healthcare data‑breach campaigns in recent history. Khlynovskiy is charged with conspiracy to commit extortion and other related offenses.
