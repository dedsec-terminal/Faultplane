### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers uncovered 13 malicious Composer theme packages on Packagist that inject JavaScript into Vietnamese movie and comic streaming sites. The injected code targets unpatched iOS devices, deploying spyware that steals crypto wallet seeds while also running mobile ad‑fraud and gambling‑redirect operations.
