### Executive Summary
Google Chrome has introduced new scam protection features that leverage Gemini AI to identify deceptive websites and alert users to potential phishing. The update also warns users when they copy suspicious commands from ClickFix attacks, a known malware technique. These measures aim to reduce phishing and malware incidents by providing real‑time warnings and blocking harmful sites.
