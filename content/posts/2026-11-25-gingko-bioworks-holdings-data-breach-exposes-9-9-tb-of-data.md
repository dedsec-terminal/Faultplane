### Executive Summary
On Oct 8, Gingko Bioworks Holdings announced a data breach. On Nov 19, threat actor FulcrumSec added the breach to its clear net leak site, claiming to have exfiltrated 9.9 TB of data, 10.8 million files across 82 cloud buckets. The company has been slow to provide updates.
