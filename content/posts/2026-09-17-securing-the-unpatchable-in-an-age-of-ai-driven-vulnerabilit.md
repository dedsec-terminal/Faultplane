### Executive Summary
AI-driven vulnerability discovery is increasing, making some vulnerabilities hard or impossible to patch. To mitigate, organizations should use network segmentation, maintain rigorous visibility, and deploy next‑gen firewalls with intrusion prevention systems as compensatory controls.
