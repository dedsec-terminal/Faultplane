### Executive Summary
J.P. Morgan Chase reported a data breach caused by a cyberattack on a third‑party service provider. The breach exposed personal and marketing data of customers of Chase, Visa, and Mastercard, including names, addresses, and other identifying information. The incident highlights risks of third‑party vendors in the financial sector.
