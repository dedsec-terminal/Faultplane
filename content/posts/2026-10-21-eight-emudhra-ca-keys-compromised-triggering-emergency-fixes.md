### Executive Summary
Eight private keys of eMudhra intermediate certificate authority certificates were compromised, allowing attackers to issue trusted certificates for any website. Mozilla and Google’s Chrome Root Program confirmed the breach on Oct 20, prompting emergency updates across browsers and related software.
