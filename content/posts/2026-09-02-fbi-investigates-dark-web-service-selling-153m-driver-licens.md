### Executive Summary
A new dark‑web marketplace is offering scanned copies of over 153 million U.S. and Canadian driver licenses. The images appear to have been harvested from a Louisiana‑based identity‑verification firm. The FBI’s New Orleans field office has opened an inquiry into the source of the stolen documents.
