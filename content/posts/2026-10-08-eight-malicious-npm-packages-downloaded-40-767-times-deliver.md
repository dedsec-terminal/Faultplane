### Executive Summary
Researchers uncovered a long-running npm supply‑chain attack, codenamed MALFEX, where 12 malicious packages were published since August 2023. Eight of these packages were downloaded 40,767 times and delivered the Overlord RAT and a credential stealer to infected hosts.
