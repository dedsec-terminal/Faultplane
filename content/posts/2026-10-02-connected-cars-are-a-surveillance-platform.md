### Executive Summary
Northeastern University researchers and Consumer Reports examined modern cars' data practices, revealing that vehicle telemetry, apps, and third‑party services continuously transmit driver data to insurers, lenders, data brokers, infotainment providers, and government agencies, effectively turning cars into surveillance platforms.
