Here are 3 concise paragraphs summarizing the article:

Private conversations with Anthropic's AI chatbot, Claude, were recently exposed in Google and Bing search results. The issue was first flagged by a Reddit user, who discovered that chats containing sensitive information, such as political affiliations and erotic role play, could be easily found via web search. The chats were shared by users, who created public URLs to specific chatbot threads, which were then indexed by search engines.

The problem lies in the way websites and search engines interact. Anthropic uses a robots.txt file to instruct web crawlers not to index shared chats, but this method is not foolproof. Both Bing and Google recommend using a "noindex" tag on individual pages to prevent indexing, but the exposed Claude chat pages did not include this tag. As a result, search engines were able to index the pages, making them publicly accessible.

The issue has been resolved for Google, but Bing still shows search results for shared Claude chats. Anthropic has not responded to requests for comment, while Google has stated that it is the responsibility of website owners to control what pages are made public. Users can manage access to their shared chats by going to the Claude settings, but the lack of a "noindex" tag on shared chat pages means that they could potentially be indexed again in the future.
