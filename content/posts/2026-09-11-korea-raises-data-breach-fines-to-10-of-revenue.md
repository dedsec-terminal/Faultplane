### Executive Summary
South Korea's privacy regulator will impose fines equal to 10% of a company's revenue for data breaches affecting 10 million or more individuals, aiming to incentivize preventive data protection measures. The new rule, effective Friday, targets intentional or grossly negligent leaks of personal data.
