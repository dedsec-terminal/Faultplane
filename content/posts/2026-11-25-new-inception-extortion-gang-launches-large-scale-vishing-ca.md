### Executive Summary
The Inception extortion group, a newly formed threat actor, has conducted a large-scale vishing campaign targeting the single sign‑on credentials of multiple global brands and firms. Attackers use phone-based social engineering to trick victims into revealing SSO login details, which are then used for data exfiltration or extortion. The operation demonstrates a shift toward voice‑based credential theft and highlights the vulnerability of SSO systems to social‑engineering attacks.
