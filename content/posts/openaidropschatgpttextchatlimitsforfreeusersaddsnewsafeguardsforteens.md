OpenAI has updated its ChatGPT model, introducing GPT-5.6 Sol for Plus and Pro subscribers, and GPT-5.6 Luna for free users. The new models aim to provide more focused answers, adapt to the level of detail required, and reduce unnecessary formatting. Additionally, OpenAI has removed the rate limit on text conversations for free users, allowing them to engage in unlimited chats without waiting for the limit to reset.

The update also brings new safeguards for younger users, particularly those under 18. GPT-5.6 Sol has been trained to avoid romantic roleplay, age-restricted challenges, and responses that present the assistant as a replacement for human relationships. The model also includes age-related boundaries covering sensitive topics such as sexual material, eating disorders, and graphic violence. When a teen may need support, the model encourages connection with trusted individuals.

The new models have undergone testing to evaluate their performance and resistance to adversarial prompts. OpenAI reports that GPT-5.6 Sol and Luna have shown significant improvements in reducing factual mistakes, with a 62-68% decrease in errors compared to the previous model. The models have also demonstrated strong resistance to prompt injection attacks and jailbreaks, with GPT-5.6 Sol scoring high on evaluations for connector and search and function-calling attacks. These updates aim to provide a safer and more effective chat experience for all users, including younger individuals.
