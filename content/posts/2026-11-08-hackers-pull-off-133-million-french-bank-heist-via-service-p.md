### Executive Summary
Crédit Mutuel Arkéa, a French cooperative bank, fell victim to a cyberattack that exploited a service provider, enabling attackers to execute fraudulent wire transfers totaling $133 million. The bank halted certain systems and investigated the breach, which involved compromised credentials and unauthorized access to its payment infrastructure.
