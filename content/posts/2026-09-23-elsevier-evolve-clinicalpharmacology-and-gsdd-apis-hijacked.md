### Executive Summary
Sorami Consulting reports that users and systems attempting to connect to Elsevier Evolve, Sherpath, and ClinicalPharmacology APIs are being redirected to extortion splash pages linked to the threat actor LAPSUS$. The redirects target domains such as lapsus.ar.io and lapsus.bz. The campaign has affected academic and medical institutions, causing exam and simulation charting disruptions, and represents a new form of API hijacking used for extortion.
