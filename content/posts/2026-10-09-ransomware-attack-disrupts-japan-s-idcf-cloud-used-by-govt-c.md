### Executive Summary
IDC Frontier, a major Japanese cloud provider, reported a ransomware attack on its IDCF Cloud service that caused an outage at a data‑center cluster in eastern Japan. The breach impacted several government clients, leading to service disruptions. IDC Frontier confirmed the attack, investigated the intrusion, and is working to restore services while notifying affected customers. The incident highlights the vulnerability of cloud infrastructure to ransomware threats.
