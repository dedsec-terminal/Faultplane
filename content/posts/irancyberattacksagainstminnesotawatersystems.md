Iran has been attributed to a series of cyberattacks targeting water systems in Minnesota, with preliminary reports indicating no significant damage. The attacks appear to be part of a larger campaign that has affected at least seven states. The attribution of the attacks to Iran is still preliminary, and investigations are ongoing to determine the extent of the attacks.

The incident has sparked a controversy, with President Trump downplaying the role of Iran in the attacks. Instead, he has suggested that Minnesota's own incompetence is to blame, stating that the state "hacked itself." Trump's comments have raised eyebrows, and it is unclear whether he believes the other six states targeted by the campaign have also been compromised by their own actions.

The situation has also been put into context by some observers, who note that the US has been involved in bombing municipal water systems in Iran. This has led some to suggest that the cyberattacks may be a form of retaliation. The incident highlights the complexities of cyber warfare and the potential for escalation between nations. As the investigation continues, it remains to be seen how the US will respond to the attacks and whether the attribution to Iran will be confirmed.
