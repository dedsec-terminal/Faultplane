### Executive Summary
A zero‑day vulnerability (CVE‑2026‑63073) in OpenSSL’s Certificate Management Protocol (CMP) allows remote attackers to read sensitive data via an externally‑controlled format string. No authentication is required. The flaw carries a CVSS score of 6.5.
