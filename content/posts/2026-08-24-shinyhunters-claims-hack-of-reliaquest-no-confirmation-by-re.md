### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity firm ReliaQuest has repeatedly reported on ShinyHunters activity. On Aug 17, ReliaQuest’s Twitter account announced tracking a new ShinyHunters campaign. A forum user posted screenshots on Aug 23, prompting ReliaQuest to delete the tweet and cease posting. No official confirmation of a breach has been issued by ReliaQuest, leaving the claim unverified.
