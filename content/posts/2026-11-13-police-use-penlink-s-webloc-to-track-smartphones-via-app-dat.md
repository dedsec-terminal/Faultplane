### Executive Summary
Penlink’s Webloc harvests location data from common weather, gaming, email and religious apps on smartphones, compiling a detailed 12‑month movement history that updates hourly. Law‑enforcement officers can pinpoint phones present at a specific place and time, analyzing billions of data points in near real‑time.
