Here are three concise paragraphs summarizing the week's news in cybersecurity:

The past week saw several significant developments in cybersecurity, including the discovery of vulnerabilities in various software and hardware platforms. The OWASP GenAI Security Project released its 2026 Top 10 list for LLM Applications, which highlighted the risks of prompt injection and sensitive information disclosure. Finally, experts warned of the challenges posed by the increasing volume of security patches, with the August 2026 Patch Tuesday forecast expected to be significant.
