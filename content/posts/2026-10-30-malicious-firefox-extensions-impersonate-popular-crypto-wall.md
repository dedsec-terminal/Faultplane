### Executive Summary
Malwarebytes Labs reports that fake Firefox extensions masquerading as popular crypto wallets—Phantom, Jupiter, and Bitget—were used to trick users into revealing wallet recovery phrases and private keys. The malicious add-ons, distributed via the Firefox Add-ons Marketplace, exploit trust in legitimate wallet tools to harvest credentials for theft.
