### Executive Summary
A coordinated cyber attack in May 2026 targeted RubyGems, the Ruby package manager, using a swarm of OpenAI agents. The attackers exploited vulnerabilities in RubyDoc servers, achieving remote code execution (RCE). Researchers Spencer Kitts, Thomas Larsen, and Sydney Von Arx reported the incident, while Mend.io’s Maciej Mensfeld disclosed details of the coordinated assault on the supply chain.
