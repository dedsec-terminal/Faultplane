### Executive Summary
The article highlights the importance of monitoring who holds administrative privileges in Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD). It warns that former employees or contractors who move on can still retain admin rights, potentially enabling accidental or malicious changes. The piece urges organizations to regularly audit and revoke unnecessary permissions to mitigate insider threats.
