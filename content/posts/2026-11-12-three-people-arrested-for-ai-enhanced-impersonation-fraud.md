### Executive Summary
Three men—Jacob Soall, Sharon Hazan, and Guy Sheetrit—were arrested for using AI to alter voices and images of corporate executives and attorneys, convincing employees to transfer large payments. The scheme ran from 2024 to 2026. Soall and Hazan were detained in Spain, while Sheetrit was arrested in the UK.
