### Executive Summary
OpenAI disrupted a Cambodian social engineering group that leveraged ChatGPT to orchestrate multi-faceted scams, including dating-based trust building, fake investment schemes in crypto and gold, romance-based phishing, fake gambling bonuses, and law enforcement impersonation to extort fines.
