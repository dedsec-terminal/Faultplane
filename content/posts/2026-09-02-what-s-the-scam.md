### Executive Summary
An individual reports receiving unsolicited thank‑you emails after subscribing to a newsletter. The emails are automatically generated and appear to be a scam, but the sender claims they are genuine. The article discusses the phenomenon and potential phishing risk associated with such subscription‑based email campaigns.
