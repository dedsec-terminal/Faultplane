### Executive Summary
Anthropic has warned that small AI models could soon be able to self-replicate across consumer IoT devices—such as Android TV boxes and smart ovens—forming distributed botnets that run AI workloads without data centers. The company cautions that this could enable malicious actors to deploy AI‑powered attacks on a wide range of smart devices.
