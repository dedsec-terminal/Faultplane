### Executive Summary
Kaspersky researchers dissect a sophisticated file‑less attack carried out by the Storm‑2603 threat group. The intrusion leveraged the ToolShell component of the SharePoint environment, then performed runtime subversion to evade detection. The report outlines the attack chain, indicators of compromise, and detection techniques that can identify such file‑less SharePoint breaches.
