Here is a summary of the article in three concise paragraphs:

Searchlight Cyber has launched its Preemptive Threat Exposure Management (PTEM) platform, which combines exposure visibility with real-world attacker intelligence to help organizations prioritize and reduce exposures that are most likely to be exploited. The platform is designed to address the shrinking time gap between exposure and exploitation, driven by the accelerating pace of AI-powered vulnerability discovery, exploit development, and attack execution. This shift has created a need for preemptive cybersecurity solutions, which are expected to account for 50% of IT security spending by 2030.

The Searchlight PTEM platform provides continuous exposure visibility, attack surface discovery, and exploitability validation, enabling organizations to understand what is exposed and what attackers could exploit. It also delivers real-world attacker intelligence, revealing what threat actors are actively discussing, developing, and targeting across the open, deep, and dark web. By combining exposure intelligence with observable attacker behavior, the platform helps security teams prioritize remediation using both technical risk and attacker reality, allowing them to reduce exposure before attackers act.

The launch of the PTEM platform marks a significant evolution in Searchlight Cyber's history, bringing together the company's heritage in threat intelligence with market-leading exposure management capabilities. The platform is designed to help organizations operate preemptively, rather than reactively, and is supported by a new corporate identity and redesigned web presence. The company's solutions, including Searchlight Exposure and Searchlight Threat, are now unified under a single vision, delivering exposure visibility and attacker activity context that enable preemptive action against cyber threats.
