### Executive Summary
An attacker used stolen staff passwords at France’s tax administration to steal tax data of hundreds of thousands of taxpayers and businesses in June and July. The breach went unnoticed for seven weeks, with neither the tax authority nor ANSSI detecting the data exfiltration. The attack was simple, exploiting weak credentials.
