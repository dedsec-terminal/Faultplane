### Executive Summary
Modern messaging apps let users link phone accounts to desktop clients. German customs officers have used this feature to connect a police‑controlled computer to a suspect’s WhatsApp or Signal account, enabling them to receive messages without decrypting them. The police can gain access via physical phone possession, state‑sanctioned phishing to intercept verification codes, or telephone surveillance to capture SMS messages.
