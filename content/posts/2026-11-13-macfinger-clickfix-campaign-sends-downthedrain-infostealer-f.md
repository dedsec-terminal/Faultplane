### Executive Summary
The Macfinger ClickFix campaign has been observed delivering the DownTheDRAIN infostealer to macOS systems. DownTheDRAIN harvests credentials, browser data, and other sensitive information, then exfiltrates it to command‑and‑control servers. The campaign leverages the ClickFix downloader to install the malware, enabling attackers to compromise multiple Mac users in a single operation.
