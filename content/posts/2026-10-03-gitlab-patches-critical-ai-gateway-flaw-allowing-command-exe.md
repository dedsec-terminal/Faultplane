### Executive Summary
GitLab has released patches for a critical flaw in its AI Gateway that could allow a logged‑in user with Duo Agent Platform access to execute arbitrary commands on the gateway under certain conditions. The vulnerability affects self‑hosted GitLab instances and is fixed in gateway versions 19.2.4, 19.3.2, and 19.4.1.
