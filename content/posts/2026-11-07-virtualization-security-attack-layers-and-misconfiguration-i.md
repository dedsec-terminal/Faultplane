### Executive Summary
The Securelist article examines how misconfigurations in virtualized environments—specifically VMware ESXi and Proxmox—create attack surfaces that ransomware actors exploit. It details real-world incidents where attackers leveraged default credentials, exposed management interfaces, and insecure network segmentation to deploy ransomware payloads. The piece outlines best‑practice hardening steps, such as disabling unused services, enforcing strong authentication, and isolating management traffic, to mitigate these risks.
