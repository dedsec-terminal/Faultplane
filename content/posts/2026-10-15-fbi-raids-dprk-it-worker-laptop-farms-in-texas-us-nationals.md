The U.S. Department of Justice disrupted the Famous Chollima threat group by arresting two suspects, charging 14 individuals, and seizing nearly 187 laptops used in North Korean remote IT worker schemes. The FBI raided laptop farms in Texas, targeting U.S. nationals involved in the operation.
