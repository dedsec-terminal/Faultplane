### Executive Summary
A $26 million cyber heist targeted the historic New Jersey town of Cranbury, a community founded in 1697. Attackers gained unauthorized access to local financial systems, siphoning funds and exposing sensitive data. The breach underscores the vulnerability of small-town infrastructure to sophisticated cybercrime and the significant financial impact such attacks can impose.
