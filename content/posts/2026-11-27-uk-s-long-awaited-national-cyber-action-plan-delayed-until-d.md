### Executive Summary

Britain has again delayed the launch of its National Cyber Action Plan, the government’s long-awaited strategy for defending the wider economy against state-backed and criminal hackers, according to multiple people familiar with the matter. The document, originally conceived as a national cyber strategy, was first due to be published by the end of 2025. After initial delays it was rebranded as an action plan and was close to publication in July this year before being postponed amid Keir Starmer’s resignation. Officials, who had planned to publish the document in mid-November, now aim to unveil it on December 9 according to several sources, speaking to Recorded Future News on condition of anonymity.
